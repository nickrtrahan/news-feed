World News Brief: Nov 3, AM

Iran and the Gulf

U.S. strikes Qeshm Island. The U.S. carried out what officials called limited "defensive" strikes on Iranian targets on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, a U.S. official said (AP, 8:55 PM ET; Reuters, 10:00 PM ET; BBC, 10:42 PM ET). AFP, citing U.S. media, said Iranian media reported five explosions in quick succession (AFP, 4:54 AM ET). The Wall Street Journal reportedly described the strikes as aimed at Iranian missiles that threatened U.S. aircraft. Iranian media also reported blasts in Bandar Lengeh and Ahvaz, and some analysts claimed Israel was behind at least some of them; that attribution is unconfirmed and conflicts with the U.S. officials' account (X story summary).

Iranian drone claims (unconfirmed). The Revolutionary Guard says its air defenses shot down two U.S.-made LUCAS one-way attack drones near Bandar Khamir on Tuesday, sharing photos of apparent wreckage. There has been no U.S. response and the claim is not independently confirmed (X story summary). Viral posts declaring that "the war has started" are commentary, not official statements (X story summary).

Talks: deal still out of reach. An Iranian official told Reuters an Iran–U.S. deal is still out of reach despite the diplomatic push (Reuters, 6:35 AM ET). Parliament Speaker and chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf told lawmakers Iran has given a "strong and rational" response and that preventing war is now up to Washington, warning forces have their "finger on the trigger" (X story summary). Indirect talks are being mediated by Qatar; Foreign Minister Araghchi told Al Jazeera that talks with Saudi Arabia are ongoing and called for regional security arrangements without foreign troops (X story summary). ISW says senior Iranian officials met Putin and senior Chinese officials on Nov. 2 (ISW, 1:00 AM ET).

Markets. Oil rebounded after the strikes, a day after one of its biggest drops of the conflict (Reuters, 1:00 AM ET; Reuters, 6:55 AM ET). Gulf stock markets were mixed as Iran–Saudi talks offset fears (Reuters, 4:40 AM ET), and Aramco reported third-quarter profit up by a third despite attacks (Reuters, 6:10 AM ET).

Executions. Iran executed two young men, Amir Hossein Ebrahimi and Amir Hossein Maleki, at Isfahan's Dastgerd Prison over the January protests; rights groups allege torture and forced confessions, and Reza Pahlavi condemned the killings (X story summary). Pahlavi begins a Belgium–Germany trip and accepts Germany's Freedom Prize in Frankfurt on Nov. 7 (X story summary).

Russia–Ukraine

Biggest attack on Ukraine's energy this year. Russia launched its largest mass attack on Ukraine's energy system this year, including one of the biggest waves of ballistic missiles plus cruise missiles and drones, according to grid operator Ukrenergo (Kyiv Independent, 6:09 AM ET). A Russian missile hit a medical facility in Kharkiv, with roughly 200 patients being evacuated (Kyiv Independent, 10:52 PM ET); later tallies put the injured at 73. In Dnipro, a hospice for the terminally ill elderly was hit, with one killed and 17 injured reported (X story summary). AFP says the flare-up dashes hopes of progress toward peace (AFP, 2:16 AM ET).

Komyshuvakha toll rises. The death toll from Monday's strike on a school used as an aid point in Zaporizhzhia region has risen to 29, including two rescuers and four police officers, with people still missing, according to X's summary of reports; earlier, Reuters had reported 26 (X story summary).

Ukraine hits Yaroslavl refinery. Ukrainian drones reportedly struck the Slavneft-YANOS refinery in Yaroslavl overnight, causing several fires (Kyiv Independent, 9:52 PM ET). It's described as the fifth major strike on Russian oil infrastructure in five days (X story summary).

Diplomacy. Zelensky arrived in Berlin for talks with Chancellor Merz on air defense and the energy sector (Kyiv Independent, 6:32 AM ET; Reuters, 6:26 AM ET). U.S.-mediated trilateral talks are set for Abu Dhabi on Nov. 10–11 (X story summary). The European Commission publishes its enlargement reports today, expected to include accession roadmaps for leading candidates including Ukraine (Kyiv Independent, 2:34 AM ET). Troops from about 10 European countries will hold the first exercise of a new multinational force for Ukraine in Poland from Nov. 9 (AFP, 9:23 PM ET).
