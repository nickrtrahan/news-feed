World News Brief: Nov 3, PM

Iran and the Gulf

Trump: no immediate plan to strike. President Trump told the Financial Times the U.S. has no immediate plan to strike Iran (Reuters, 7:00 PM ET). That follows overnight U.S. strikes on Qeshm Island, which a U.S. official described as limited and not a resumption of major combat operations; Fox News, citing officials, called them defensive (Times of Israel, 9:00 AM ET).

Talks: Tehran says no deal is near. Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmail Baghaei said on state TV that message exchanges through Qatari mediators continue with no new steps toward agreement, and security official Mohsen Rezaei called the U.S. response to Iran's seven conditions unconvincing, demanding they be met before the Strait of Hormuz reopens (X story summary). Earlier reports said the U.S. had approved a Qatari-brokered draft trading nuclear concessions for the release of some frozen funds; that draft is reported, not officially confirmed (X story summary).

IRGC warning and threats. The Revolutionary Guard said it is carrying out Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei's directives for "maximum deterrence," with updated target lists and responses "without geographic limits"; Italy urged its citizens to leave Iran immediately (X story summary). Iran's Fars news agency published a "target" list that included hotels in Doha and Abu Dhabi used by Formula 1 and MotoGP teams; Yamaha reportedly pulled its MotoGP teams from Qatar, while F1 says its races remain scheduled (X story summary). A billboard in Tehran's Revolution Square shows Trump behind bars with the words "We Will Kill Trump" (X story summary).

Israeli chemical-weapons warning (disputed). Israel has reportedly told U.S. officials that Iran may use chemical weapons, but some U.S. and Gulf officials are skeptical of the evidence and Iran denies it. Reports that U.S. strike options could be ready as early as Nov. 4 are unconfirmed (X story summary).

Drone near Minab (unconfirmed). Footage showed drone wreckage near Minab, which Iranian sources first called a downed U.S. Switchblade. Analysts say it more likely matches an Iranian one-way attack drone, possibly hit by friendly fire; neither government has commented (X story summary).

Russia–Ukraine

Komyshuvakha toll rises to 33. Zelensky said in Berlin that 33 people were killed in Russian strikes on a school used as an aid center in Zaporizhzhia region, and more than 50 may still be trapped under rubble, calling it one of Moscow's worst attacks of the war (Kyiv Independent, 3:25 PM ET; AFP, 5:00 PM ET). Russian attacks across Ukraine killed 38 and injured 158 overall, including morning missile strikes on Dnipro hospitals (Kyiv Independent, 7:37 AM ET; BBC, 11:40 AM ET). Kyiv is reported to have rolling outages leaving about 2.5 hours of power a day (X story summary).

Diplomacy. Standing with Chancellor Merz, Zelensky said talks with Putin are pointless without a ceasefire first (X story summary). The European Commission hosts talks in Brussels on Nov. 4 with the U.S., Ukraine, NATO and European states to coordinate ahead of planned talks with Russia (Kyiv Independent, 11:45 AM ET), and U.S.-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi remain set for Nov. 10–11. Melania Trump will host Ukrainian and Russian officials at the White House over the Oleshky evacuation ceasefire she helped broker (Kyiv Independent, 9:02 AM ET). In Minsk, Lukashenko predicted the war will end in coming months, while Lavrov rejected limited truces (X story summary).
