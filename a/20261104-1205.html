World News Brief: Nov 4, AM

Top line

Democrats took control of both the House and Senate in Tuesday's U.S. midterms, and President Trump said the Iran war was part of the reason. Around the Strait of Hormuz, Iran's Revolutionary Guards claim four tankers are burning after hitting mines, while Trump says the U.S. has no immediate plan to strike Iran. In Ukraine, the fallout continues from a massive Russian strike on the power grid, ahead of U.S.-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi next week. Covers roughly 7 PM ET Tuesday to 7 AM ET Wednesday, Nov 4.

Iran and Israel–Iran

  • Tankers reported ablaze near Hormuz (Iranian claim). Reuters reported at 11:00 PM ET Tuesday, and again at 3:10 AM ET, that the IRGC says four tankers caught fire after hitting mines near the Strait of Hormuz (Reuters). This is Iran's account and hasn't been independently confirmed. Separately, the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency (UKMTO) warned of a tanker struck twice in the engine room about 14 nautical miles off Lima, Oman, around 2 PM ET Tuesday, with no casualties or spills reported, according to an X news summary. The IRGC also claims to have shot down another U.S. MQ-9 Reaper drone. That claim is unconfirmed.
  • Trump: no immediate plan to strike Iran. Trump told the Financial Times the U.S. has no immediate plan to strike Iran (Reuters, 11:40 PM ET).
  • Trump links the Iran war to GOP midterm losses. "I think a part of it was Iran," Trump told Fox News (Reuters, 6:45 AM ET). The Times of Israel quotes him saying "people weren't willing to pay more for gasoline" (Times of Israel).
  • U.S. to end annual aid to Israel. Trump says he made a deal with Netanyahu to stop the roughly $4 billion in yearly U.S. funding to Israel after this year, while continuing weapons sales (Reuters, 10:58 PM ET). AFP puts the figure at $3.8 billion a year (AFP).
  • Selective Hormuz passage. An Iranian lawmaker says ships from India, China, Iraq and Qatar are exempt from Iran's transit restrictions, with Qatari ships passing fee-free, according to an X news summary. That's a lawmaker's statement, not a confirmed government policy. Meanwhile, Reuters reports India's refiner MRPL is buying more delivered oil because of shipping risks (Reuters).
  • Gulf threats hit motorsport. Iran's Fars news agency published a "target" list that names hotels in Doha and Abu Dhabi used by F1 and MotoGP teams. Yamaha has reportedly pulled its MotoGP teams from Qatar, while F1 is keeping its schedule for now, according to an X news summary. These reports aren't confirmed.
  • Inside Iran. Iran executed two men tied to January protests in Isfahan, bringing executions in that case to nine, according to an X news summary. On Wednesday, state-organized rallies marked the anniversary of the 1979 U.S. Embassy seizure (X news summary). Billboards in Tehran threatening Trump were also reported.
  • Israeli politics. President Herzog accepted the certified results of the Oct. 27 election and will begin consultations on forming a government (AP, 5:57 AM ET). Israel's electoral authorities rejected Netanyahu's allegations of fraud by Arab parties (AFP, 5:55 AM ET).

Russia–Ukraine

  • Grid strike fallout. ISW describes the large combined drone and missile strike on Ukrainian energy infrastructure overnight Nov 2–3 as part of a campaign to coerce Ukraine (ISW, 12:05 AM ET). According to an X news summary, power plants were damaged in several regions, a hospice in Dnipro was hit (nearly 50 patients evacuated), and civilian deaths reached about 38. The same summary says Kyiv is down to about 2.5 hours of power a day. Those casualty and outage figures haven't been checked against a wire report.
  • Ukrainian strikes inside Russia. Online retailer Ozon says a drone hit one of its logistics centers in Russia's Adygea Republic overnight (Kyiv Independent). The Russian-run management of the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant says a Ukrainian drone killed two people, including a station employee (Reuters, 7:45 PM ET). That claim comes from the Russian side only.
  • Diplomacy. In Berlin with Chancellor Merz, Zelensky said there's no point in talks with Putin without a ceasefire first. U.S.-brokered talks are set for Abu Dhabi on Nov 10–11 (X news summary). Britain and France are preparing a post-ceasefire stabilization force, which will rehearse in Exercise Koalicja in Poland from Nov 9–22 (X news summary).
  • Other items. Zelensky awarded Melania Trump the Order of Princess Olga for humanitarian work (Kyiv Independent). Canada will block Russia from the World Junior Hockey Championship despite a court ruling (Kyiv Independent). Ukraine's military estimates 2,180 Russian personnel losses in the past day (Kyiv Independent). That's a Ukrainian figure that can't be independently verified.

U.S. midterms (world impact)

  • Democrats will control both chambers of Congress (Reuters). Closer to home, Reuters reports Democrat Annie Andrews won South Carolina's U.S. Senate seat in an upset (Reuters, 5:50 AM ET).
  • Other notable upsets include Gina Hinojosa defeating Texas Gov. Abbott (Reuters) and Adam Hamilton beating Sen. Roger Marshall in Kansas (AP). Keisha Lance Bottoms was projected to win Georgia's governor race (Reuters).
