World News Brief: Nov 20, PM

Top line

Friday evening, Nov 20, covering roughly 6 AM to 6:40 PM ET. The Strait of Hormuz flared again: a tanker was hit near Oman and something exploded over Iran's Qeshm Island, with conflicting accounts of what happened. In Ukraine, Russia's overnight barrage hit three Kyiv bridges and killed at least nine, and two Russian missiles likely came down in Poland.

Iran and Israel–Iran

Tanker hit near Oman. An oil tanker leaving the Strait of Hormuz was struck by a projectile on its port bow about eight nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, starting a fire on board, the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency said. It reported no casualties (Iran International, citing UKMTO, 11:15 AM ET). Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy said the ship "hit a mine" after trying an unauthorized route (Iran International, 11:45 AM ET). Who or what hit the ship has not been independently confirmed.

Explosion at Qeshm Island. The IRGC-affiliated Tasnim agency said a missile struck the outskirts of Qeshm city at about 10:20 AM ET (6:50 PM local), about 20 minutes after the tanker was hit (Iran International). A Hormozgan provincial official later said the explosions came from controlled disposal of unexploded American munitions from earlier attacks (Iran International, 11:50 AM ET). Unconfirmed: posts circulating on X describe this as a U.S. missile strike in response to Iranian cruise-missile launches. I found no U.S. confirmation.

Iranian tankers and the blockade. Fishermen filmed a fully loaded tanker near the strait. The IRGC-affiliated Sabereen News, in a post it called "unofficial," said dozens of Iranian tankers are being loaded to test the U.S. naval blockade (Iran International, 9:37 AM ET). A lawmaker urged Tehran to take an "aggressive stance" to break the blockade (Iran International). Satellite counts of 14+ tankers near Kharg are reported on X but not verified here.

Washington. President Trump said the U.S. Navy has "total control" of the strait and that oil had fallen to near $80 a barrel (Iran International, 1:33 PM ET). He said "Iran will never have a nuclear weapon" (1:03 PM ET) and said the U.S. would not let Iran build a rumored "dirty bomb" (5:32 PM ET). He called JD Vance's view that the Iran war cost Republicans in the midterms "fine," but said he would launch the war again if needed (1:31 PM ET). The State Department said economic pressure on Iran will increase in the coming days (3:31 PM ET). Separately, the Treasury pressed Singapore and Malaysia on sanctions enforcement (Reuters, 2:00 PM ET).

Diplomacy. Axios reported that envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met senior Qatari officials in Berlin Thursday about reviving the ceasefire and nuclear talks (Iran International, 2:09 PM ET). Foreign Minister Araghchi said Tehran's 48-hour proposal to reopen the strait and start talks with Washington "remains on the table" (5:14 PM ET). The EU and Britain jointly condemned Iran's attacks on neighbors and shipping (8:37 AM ET). Germany's Chancellor Merz demanded the strait be permanently reopened (4:18 PM ET).

Possible strike planning. Unconfirmed reports circulating on X say U.S. and Israeli forces are positioned for a possible joint strike within days if ordered. They also say Trump has not authorized one. Treat these as speculation.

Inside Iran. Protesters marched in Mormori, near Abdanan in Ilam Province, Friday evening, chanting pro-monarchy slogans, according to video sent to Iran International (4:07 PM ET). The unrest follows a deadly Nov. 14 bus crash nearby. Human rights activists say Iran's Supreme Court upheld death sentences for two of the young men known as the "Ekbatan Boys," arrested in the 2022 protests. An Iranian military official said the armed forces had declared some outlets, Iran International among them, to be "targets" (6:05 AM ET).

Russia–Ukraine

Kyiv bridges hit. Russia fired dozens of missiles and hundreds of drones overnight, hours after Putin promised a "painful" response to Ukraine's strikes on the Kerch (Crimean) Bridge (AFP, 5:50 AM ET). Three Kyiv bridges over the Dnipro were struck and at least nine people were killed (Kyiv Independent). Russia also targeted what the Kyiv Independent called Europe's largest substation (Kyiv Independent, 2:10 PM ET).

Missiles in Poland. Poland said two Russian missiles likely landed in its southeast corner (AFP, 9:00 AM ET). The Kyiv Independent identified them as Kh-101s, along with a drone (2:10 PM ET). Estonia said it will close its road borders with Russia (Kyiv Independent).

Ukraine hits back. Zelensky said Ukrainian forces struck three Russian ballistic missile launchers, destroying one, plus an air defense system (Kyiv Independent, 10:25 AM ET). Ukraine's General Staff confirmed a strike on a Druzhba oil pipeline station in Tatarstan. Reuters reports that the Crimean bridge strikes are straining Russian logistics (Reuters, 7:35 AM ET). Not independently confirmed: reports of a Ukrainian counteroffensive, "Operation Bear Trap," that retook 14 settlements in northern Kharkiv.

Diplomacy. After the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in China, Trump said he and Putin had "very good talks" and that Putin "expressed his desire" to end the war. He said he would not discuss security guarantees for Ukraine without a deal (Reuters, 3:50 PM ET; AFP). Reuters' Steve Holland said the summit produced no major breakthrough on Ukraine or Iran (Reuters). ISW says Putin again rejected European security guarantees and is using the Kerch strikes to distract from his own refusal to negotiate (ISW, 10:00 AM ET). The EU approved €1 billion in military aid (Kyiv Independent) and paid out almost €8 billion for reforms (Kyiv Independent). Officials told the Kyiv Independent that a 22nd sanctions package is expected Nov. 23 (Kyiv Independent). Zelensky said Ukraine needs $115 billion in outside funding for 2027.

Other conflicts and world news

  • Iraq: Iraqi security forces deployed into Jurf al-Sakhar, a stronghold of the Iran-backed Kataib Hezbollah south of Baghdad, after the prime minister's deadline over alleged oil smuggling (Reuters, 9:30 AM ET). Reports say Iran's Quds Force commander Esmail Qaani brokered the deal. That is unconfirmed.
  • Gaza: Palestinian sources report at least seven people killed Friday, including four in a drone strike in central Gaza, despite the ceasefire. Israel says it is striking Hamas regrouping. Winter floods are damaging shelters. These figures come from X news summaries and are not independently confirmed.
  • Somalia: Somalia's president accused the UAE of making port and security deals with Somaliland, Puntland and Jubaland without federal approval, and reportedly expelled the UAE ambassador. The UAE called the move escalatory. Based on X news summaries.
  • Sudan: Catholic bishops warn of threats to Christian communities in the war between the army and the RSF. Videos alleging kidnappings of Christians are circulating but are unverified.
  • Ukraine's children: UNICEF says at least 1,144 children have been killed or injured in Ukraine this year (Kyiv Independent). Nov. 20 is World Children's Day.

Sources: posts from Reuters, AFP, Kyiv Independent, ISW and Iran International on X, plus X news story summaries. Items marked unconfirmed have not been independently verified.
