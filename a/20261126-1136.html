World News Brief: Nov 26, AM

Thursday, Nov 26, 2026 — morning edition, covering roughly 6:30 PM ET Wednesday to 6:30 AM ET Thursday. Happy Thanksgiving.

Iran and Israel–Iran

US confirms direct contacts with Tehran, keeps options open. US envoy Jared Kushner said he has met Iranian officials directly, working under Vice President JD Vance, and that President Trump is keeping all options open on Iran (Al Jazeera, 8:01 PM ET Wed). No details of any outcome have been released.

Israel signals readiness to strike. IDF spokesman Effie Defrin told Kan that Israel is "ready to strike with force at any moment," even without the US (Iran International, 1:25 AM ET; Al Jazeera, 5:20 AM ET). The Times of Israel reported that Netanyahu will convene his security cabinet Thursday evening amid Israeli and US preparations for a possible operation against Iran (via Iran International, 10:01 PM ET Wed). Opposition figure Gadi Eisenkot is warning against renewed strikes (Iran International).

Earlier claim, still unverified: Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz said Iranian sleeper cells in the US had been ordered to target Trump and senior officials; US authorities have not confirmed this, and Iran's FM Araghchi rejected it (Araghchi on X).

Strait of Hormuz and oil. Iran's IRGC says two oil tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines near Oman's Khasab port (Al Jazeera, 6:01 AM ET) — this is an IRGC claim, not independently confirmed. Oil prices rose on the reports and on Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia (Reuters, 2:50 AM ET). On Wednesday Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called Iran's regime "a sinking ship," saying it has not loaded a single tanker since Operation Economic Outcast began in August (Bessent on X, 9:09 AM ET Wed).

Proxies preparing. ISW reports Kataib Hezbollah has moved equipment and set up new positions in western Anbar and Karbala in preparation for a possible renewed US–Iran war (ISW, 7:43 PM ET Wed); Iraqi media say Iran-backed groups are preparing to hit US assets if Washington attacks Iran (Al Jazeera).

Nuclear. A Washington-based research institute says Iran is quickly building a fortified underground complex between two main nuclear sites (Iran International, 5:28 AM ET). Iran's atomic energy chief Mohammad Eslami called weapons claims fabricated (Al Jazeera).

Inside Iran. The nationwide truckers' strike over costs, which began Nov 22, continued; drivers refused to load cargo at Imam Khomeini port Thursday, per footage sent to Iran International (Iran International, 6:29 AM ET). The US State Department said at least 135 lawyers were arrested, summoned or banned from travel in the past year (Iran International). The lawyer for two Ekbatan-case protesters says their executions were halted, though the judiciary insists the sentences stand (reported on X; not independently confirmed).

Russia–Ukraine

Deadly night of strikes. Russian attacks killed 10 and injured 58 across Ukraine over the past day, including ballistic missiles on Kyiv and 113 drones (Kyiv Independent, 6:32 AM ET). A Kyiv shopping center was hit Wednesday night (Kyiv Independent, 4:45 PM ET Wed). A Russian-installed official says a Ukrainian drone attack killed five in occupied Zaporizhzhia (Al Jazeera, 6:25 AM ET).

Diplomacy. Trump and NATO chief Mark Rutte, meeting in Florida, jointly urged Russia not to "expand the war" (AFP, 10:50 PM ET Wed). ISW says the latest US proposal would make Ukraine's security guarantees depend on approval by Congress and Russia, which could delay or block them (ISW, 7:10 PM ET Wed). EU leaders are discussing new winter support and long-term Ukraine financing (Kyiv Independent; Reuters).

North Korea. Ukrainian intelligence says North Korea has paused military aid to Russia, including KN-23 missile deliveries (Kyiv Independent, 4:58 AM ET) — Ukrainian intelligence claim, not independently confirmed.

Chemical weapons. More than 30 countries demanded Russia stop using toxic chemicals and riot-control agents as weapons in Ukraine (Kyiv Independent).

Refinery war and a laser first. Ukraine says it struck two Rosneft refineries in Samara region (Ukraine MoD), after Putin complained Kyiv ignored Trump's request to stop such strikes. Ukraine also says it downed a jet-powered Shahed-type drone with a laser weapon for the first time (OSINTtechnical). Separately, Meduza and Mediazona estimate from leaked Rosstat data that Russian military deaths may reach about 720,000 (an estimate; the Kremlin rejects it).

Other Conflicts

Houthis vs. Saudi Arabia. Explosions were heard over Riyadh as Saudi forces downed Houthi missiles and drones (AFP, 10:08 PM ET Wed). The Houthis claimed hits on the Ras Tanura refinery; Planet Labs satellite images shared on X show heavy smoke there (via Mario Nawfal), while Saudi officials report interceptions. Saudi Arabia is preparing to delay a flagship mining conference (FT).

Gaza. Israeli strikes killed eight Palestinians including a senior Hamas education official and his wife (BBC, 5:15 AM ET; Times of Israel), despite the ceasefire.

Sudan. Sudan's government rejected Ethiopian PM Abiy Ahmed's offer to host peace talks, citing a lack of neutrality (Sudan Tribune); the African Union Commission chair issued a statement on the proposal (African Union).

Top World News

  • Hong Kong marked one year since the Wang Fuk Court fire that killed 168, with officials warning against "disorder" (AFP).
  • UK net migration fell to its lowest level since 1997, excluding the pandemic (NYT).
