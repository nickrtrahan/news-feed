World News Brief: Nov 24, AM

Covering roughly 6:30 PM ET Monday, Nov 23 to 6:45 AM ET Tuesday, Nov 24, 2026. All times ET.

Iran and Israel–Iran

Ghalibaf sets conditions for talks. Iran's parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf told lawmakers Tuesday morning (around 1 AM ET) that there will be no negotiations with the United States until seven conditions are met. He named five publicly: ending wars on all fronts, releasing frozen assets, lifting sanctions, guaranteeing oil-sale waivers, and ending the U.S. naval blockade. He warned of a "decisive" response to any U.S. mistake (Drop Site; Press TV, Iranian state media).

Blockade standoff continues. This follows President Trump's Fox News remarks that he leans toward resuming strikes on Iran after January 1 if talks stall, and his warning that ships breaching the blockade near Hormuz would end up "at the bottom of the sea." Iran's Foreign Ministry called the blockade military aggression (X news summary; related reporting).

More Treasury sanctions. Monday afternoon (2:37 PM ET), the Treasury said it sanctioned more than two dozen Iranian exchange houses, front companies and key staff under "Operation Economic Outcast" (U.S. Treasury). Secretary Scott Bessent called Iran's shadow banking system "the last financial lifeline of a bankrupt regime" (Bessent).

Iran suspected in ship cyberattacks. The U.S. is investigating Iran as a possible culprit in dozens of recent cyberattacks on ships, the Wall Street Journal reported (Reuters; Times of Israel).

Gulf of Oman ship hit. The UK's maritime security agency issued a warning (3:43 AM ET) about a bulk carrier struck by an unknown projectile about 23 nautical miles northeast of Khor Fakkan, UAE, on Nov 22. A fire broke out; no injuries were reported, and no one has claimed it (UKMTO; OSINT summary).

Unconfirmed: tanker and jet claims. Iran's IRGC claimed three tankers hit mines under U.S. air escort, and flight trackers showed a U.S. F-35 squawking an emergency code over the UAE Monday afternoon before landing at Al Dhafra. Neither has been independently confirmed, and U.S. Central Command has not commented. Pro-Iran accounts' claims of a shootdown are unverified (Hormuz Report, flight-tracking).

Oil slips anyway. Oil touched three-month lows Tuesday morning as rising supply offset the Hormuz threat (Reuters).

Israel's Iran debate. Prime Minister Netanyahu urged a unity government to face "the great threats around us" (Netanyahu). Gadi Eisenkot, who is trying to form Israel's next coalition, was reported as saying Iran poses no immediate threat requiring war (Iran Observer; check the full quote). In a New York Magazine profile, Jared Kushner reportedly warned Israel risks "global-pariah status" (report via Mario Nawfal).

Russia–Ukraine

Zaporizhzhia attack. A Russian attack overnight killed two people in Zaporizhzhia and wounded 40, including four children, Governor Mykhailo Semikin said (Reuters; Kyiv Independent).

Ukraine sinks Russian ship in Libya. President Zelensky confirmed that Ukraine sank a Russian naval corvette in a Libyan port using sea drones, the day after a drone strike on a Russian military-linked cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland (Reuters). Kyiv's foreign ministry called the Libya strike lawful self-defense (Kyiv Independent).

Patriot plea. Zelensky warned that Russia is preparing new strikes on energy and civilian sites and asked the U.S. and partners to speed up Patriot interceptor deliveries (video of the address). The Trump administration reportedly wants a peace deal before the new Congress is seated in early January (X news summary; details unconfirmed).

Russian aircraft near South Korea. Several Russian military aircraft entered South Korea's air defense identification zone, prompting a brief alert on an eastern island. AP said this corrects earlier reports that North Korean planes were involved (AP; Reuters).

Spillover in Europe. Poland issued an hour-long air-attack alert in parts of the Lublin region Monday evening after a Russian drone violated its airspace (Kyiv Independent). Moldovan media reported a drone crashed and caught fire in Transnistria (Kyiv Independent). NATO's chief vowed to defend against space threats after reports of a Russian nuclear satellite (BBC).

Sanctions and losses. Ukraine welcomed a newly proposed EU sanctions package, which includes a proposed ban on Russian ex-combatants entering the EU (Kyiv Independent). Mediazona and Meduza, analyzing leaked Russian death records, counted at least about 490,000 war-linked deaths and estimate the true toll at roughly 700,000 (Mediazona).

Unconfirmed: A Ukrainian journalist claims Russia warned the U.S. about a supposed spring 2027 Ukrainian offensive on Crimea. No government has confirmed it (NOELreports).

Other Conflicts

Gaza. An Oxfam and Conflict and Environment Observatory report says contamination affects more than 99% of Gaza's land (Al Jazeera English). UNRWA mourned staff member Adel al-Helou, killed in an Israeli strike in Jabalia (Gaza Notifications, partisan source).

Yemen (unconfirmed blame). An attack Monday on buses and trucks on the Ma'baq road in Lahj governorate killed and wounded dozens of civilians. Houthi-aligned officials blame Saudi airstrikes, while local government authorities blame Houthi drones or artillery. Casualty figures are unverified (Houthi official's account).

Iraq. Iraq's prime minister ordered the federal oil ministry to take over oilfields in areas under Kurdish security control after an oil-smuggling probe (Reuters).
