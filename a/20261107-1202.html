Iran and Israel–Iran

  • Hormuz tanker. Iran's navy said Saturday that an oil tanker struck a naval mine and caught fire in the eastern Strait of Hormuz after entering an area Tehran had declared off-limits, saying it ignored warnings and switched off its navigation systems (Iran International, 5:03 AM ET). The tanker's name, flag, cargo, and crew status have not been released, and there is no independent confirmation yet—treat this as Iran's account only (X news summary).
  • Isfahan blasts. At least four powerful explosions were felt near Isfahan around 1:49 AM ET, near military sites hit in earlier U.S.–Israeli strikes. Iranian officials and local media called them scheduled controlled detonations of unexploded ordnance; online claims of a new U.S. attack are unsupported so far (X news summary). Reuters late Friday again cited Tasnim saying two missiles struck Qeshm Island (Reuters, 10:30 PM ET). Viral videos of huge tank fires billed as U.S. strikes on Iran actually appear to show a Ukrainian drone strike on Russia's Afipsky refinery, according to X fact-checking summaries (X news summary).
  • Talks. Qatar said its mediation between Washington and Tehran continues and is aimed at preventing escalation, and it denied social-media claims that the talks had collapsed (Reuters, 4:00 AM ET; X news summary). Trump said the U.S. has made "a lot of progress" on Iran, does not consider the confrontation a war, and said U.S. forces destroyed an Iranian-made air-defense system within minutes (Iran International, 3:56 AM ET). Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said he spoke with 12 foreign ministers in 48 hours, urging regional security without outside interference (Iran International, 2:25 AM ET). Hardline lawmakers pushed back: Abbas Golrou said negotiating is pointless because the U.S. "wants to take everything and give nothing," and Mahmoud Nabavian demanded changes to Iran's negotiating team (Golrou, 3:55 AM ET; Nabavian, 6:18 AM ET).
  • Threats and nuclear signaling. IRGC spokesman Hossein Mohebbi said the Guards would unveil previously undisclosed capabilities if a third round of fighting broke out, and would make "necessary preparations" if leaders changed Iran's nuclear doctrine (Iran International, 1:47 AM ET; nuclear doctrine, 2:43 AM ET). Lawmaker Ghasem Ravanbakhsh threatened infrastructure in Saudi Arabia and Europe if they back renewed strikes (Iran International, 3:02 AM ET). Reports of iodine-tablet distribution near nuclear sites continue, though Iran's Health Ministry has denied the official's comments behind the story (X news summary). Lebanese lawmaker Alain Aoun warned renewed U.S.–Iran fighting could drag Lebanon back into war (Iran International, 4:50 AM ET).
  • Inside Iran and the diaspora. Iran executed Baluch militant Mohammad Aref Shahbakhsh over an attack on a police station in Sistan-Baluchestan (Reuters, 4:20 AM ET). Rights group HRANA said a Tehran court sentenced January protester Hossein Darabi to 25 years for "cooperating with hostile states" (Iran International, 6:58 AM ET). Exiled crown prince Reza Pahlavi accepted Germany's Friedrich Naumann Foundation Freedom Prize on behalf of the Iranian people in Frankfurt as large crowds rallied in the city (Iran International, 6:28 AM ET). Iran International also repeated Israel's Channel 12 report that Netanyahu and the Mossad chief planned and then dropped a pre-election Iran operation after an operative's error tipped off Washington—still anonymous sourcing with no official confirmation (Iran International, 5:13 AM ET).

Russia–Ukraine

  • Kyiv strike. Hours after Zelenskyy's warning, Russian ballistic missiles hit Kyiv overnight, damaging the Northern Bridge across the Dnipro and injuring five people, including a 14-year-old girl, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said (Kyiv Independent, 12:26 AM ET). X summaries citing Ukrainian officials put the attack at up to 36 ballistic missiles and more than 170 drones, with a kindergarten and homes damaged and power, heat, and water outages in the capital—treat the exact counts as preliminary (X news summary).
  • Grid damage. Zelenskyy said Russian attacks over the past day killed 2 and injured 37 across Ukraine and damaged substations connecting nuclear power plants to the grid in the Kyiv, Vinnytsia, Zhytomyr, Rivne, Kharkiv, and Dnipro oblasts (Kyiv Independent, 6:27 AM ET). Germany and Spain are sending generators and repair aid, according to X summaries (X news summary).
  • Ukraine's strikes. Ukraine hit sites in Russia's Oryol, Saratov, and Rostov regions overnight, including the Saratov oil refinery, according to monitoring channels (Kyiv Independent, 10:14 PM ET). Ukraine's General Staff claimed 2,280 Russian personnel losses in the past day; these are Ukrainian estimates, not independently verified (Kyiv Independent, 3:18 AM ET).
  • Talks ahead. ISW said U.S. officials are presenting a list of topics, including security guarantees and European security architecture, for the upcoming Abu Dhabi negotiations, and assessed that Russia's stated demands undercut U.S., Ukrainian, and European positions (ISW, 9:07 PM ET; more, 10:00 PM ET). EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas rejected giving Moscow a say in Europe's security after Jared Kushner outlined a broader U.S. framework (X news summary). ISW also flagged an unattributed campaign of hoax bomb threats targeting European flights, airports, and media (ISW, 10:30 PM ET). Elon Musk reposted a post calling SpaceX's free Starlink service to Ukraine early in the war "a mistake" (Kyiv Independent, 7:08 PM ET).
