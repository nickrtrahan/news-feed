World News Brief: Nov 22, AM

Top line

Sunday morning, Nov 22, covering roughly 6:30 PM Saturday to 6:30 AM ET. Arab governments condemned Iran's latest attack on Bahrain, and Iran's IRGC said it shot down another US drone near the Strait of Hormuz. In the Russia–Ukraine war, a Russian drone collapsed an apartment building in Dymer near Kyiv, with people feared trapped, and Zelensky again pressed Washington on Patriot missiles. NATO says it will defend "every inch" of Poland after Russian missiles entered its airspace.

Iran and Israel–Iran

Bahrain attack fallout. Bahrain said an Iranian attack hit an industrial facility while air defences intercepted other attacks (Reuters, 8:40 PM ET). Iran's IRGC claimed it hit US Patriot radars and other US sites in Bahrain; Bahrain described the target as a civilian industrial site, and the IRGC claim is unconfirmed. Jordan called the attack a "dangerous escalation" (Al Jazeera, 11:30 PM ET), and the UAE called it a "flagrant violation" of Bahrain's sovereignty (Al Jazeera, 4:53 AM ET).

Strait of Hormuz. ISW assessed that Iran keeps attacking ships on the US-backed route in the southern strait, likely to shake shippers' trust in US escorts and push them into Iranian waters (ISW, 8:09 PM ET). The IRGC said it shot down another US MQ-1 drone east of the strait (Al Jazeera, 3:41 AM ET); this is an Iranian claim and has not been confirmed by the US. Iran's army spokesman repeated that Iran controls the strait (Al Jazeera, 4:56 AM ET).

Iranian statements. An Iranian vice president said 623 children have been killed in the US-Israeli war on Iran (Al Jazeera, 5:36 AM ET); that figure comes from Iranian officials and is unverified. Mohsen Rezaei said the "world has changed" after what he called the war's "defeat" (Al Jazeera, 6:00 AM ET). Turkey's foreign minister said a ceasefire is needed to ease the war's economic burden (Al Jazeera, 11:15 PM ET).

Inside Iran. Two men arrested in the 2022 protests, Milad Armoun and Navid Najaran, were reportedly moved to solitary confinement at Ghezel Hesar prison, and rights groups fear their executions are imminent; residents of Tehran's Ekbatan neighborhood have been chanting against the sentences. This comes from rights groups and X reports and has not been confirmed by a major wire service.

Russia–Ukraine

Dymer strike. A Russian drone hit a five-story apartment building in Dymer, Kyiv region, overnight; Zelensky said 14 people may be trapped under the rubble (Kyiv Independent, 3:50 AM ET). Rescuers later recovered one body (Kyiv Post, 5:19 AM ET). Across Ukraine, Russian attacks injured 30 people over the past day, and the Air Force said 161 of 186 drones were shot down overnight (Kyiv Independent, 5:42 AM ET).

Patriots and diplomacy. Zelensky said US delays on Patriot missiles that allies have already paid for are "difficult to understand," after Russia fired 165 missiles and about 2,700 drones this week (Kyiv Independent, 5:57 AM ET; Kyiv Post, 5:13 AM ET). Trump told Fox News the US is working on a "larger-scale" ceasefire, building on small evacuation pauses (Kyiv Post, 4:09 AM ET). ISW noted Zelensky's proposal for local ceasefires to evacuate civilians from 15 settlements along the Dnipro (ISW, 8:45 PM ET).

Strikes inside Russia. Ukrainian drones reportedly hit a Russian National Guard (Rosgvardiya) building in Krasnodar and halted airport flights (Kyiv Independent, 10:47 PM ET; Kyiv Post, 4:43 AM ET). The exact target is based on open-source reports and is not officially confirmed. A Kaliningrad-bound Russian cargo ship hit by drones is still burning after drifting into Finnish waters (Reuters, 3:40 AM ET; Al Jazeera, 6:20 AM ET).

NATO. NATO chief Mark Rutte said the alliance "will defend every inch of Polish territory" after two Russian missiles and a drone entered Poland; NATO ambassadors meet Monday (Kyiv Post, 5:52 AM ET). Moldova and Finland also reported Russian airspace violations (Kyiv Independent, 6:46 PM ET). Britain will build Ukrainian long-range drones in the UK (Kyiv Independent, 8:48 PM ET).

Other conflicts

Gaza and West Bank. At least two Palestinians were killed in Gaza on Sunday despite the 2025 ceasefire (Al Jazeera, 3:05 AM ET). World Central Kitchen's José Andrés said three people killed in an Israeli strike on a food vehicle Saturday were not WCK staff (Al Jazeera, 12:34 AM ET). Israeli settlers reportedly burned vehicles and homes in villages near Ramallah overnight, according to local reports on X (unconfirmed by a major wire).

Lebanon. President Joseph Aoun demanded Israel withdraw from southern Lebanon, criticized Iranian interference, and pledged to put all weapons under state control (Al Jazeera, 8:15 PM ET).

Sudan. Drones hit homes, government buildings and a UN site in Khartoum early Sunday, according to witnesses and aid officials cited in X news reports; an army source blamed the RSF. This is not yet confirmed by a major wire service. Displaced families in Darfur face a hard winter as aid is cut (Al Jazeera, 1:52 AM ET).

Other world news

  • China objected to a US FCC ban on Chinese telecoms' direct access to US phone numbers (Reuters, 12:10 AM ET).
  • Thailand plans to block exports of chemicals used in Myanmar's rare-earth mining (Reuters, 3:50 AM ET).
