No final decision is reported; Vance has privately criticized Netanyahu's Iran approach (X news summary of Axios). Separately, Axios framed Trump's post-midterm Iran choices as mostly bad (Axios, 8:06 PM ET Wed), and Reuters asked whether the election rebuke makes Trump more aggressive or more cautious (Reuters podcast).

  • Iran reads the midterms as a win. ISW says Iranian leaders treat the Nov 3 results as proof their military strategy imposed political costs on Washington and will keep maximalist demands; Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei's remarks point the same way (ISW, 8:47 PM ET Wed; follow-up). Tehran billboards and state rallies have threatened Trump and others after the vote (X news summary).

UK Maritime Trade Operations said a tanker was hit by two unidentified projectiles about 50 nautical miles north of Qatar's Madinat ash Shamal around 6:20 PM ET Wed; crew status and damage were unclear (X news summary). Separately, Iranian media showed IRGC Ground Forces commander Brig. Gen. Mohammad Karami inspecting Strait of Hormuz units and vowing a crushing response to any aggression (X news summary). IRGC claims of related Hormuz fires and downed U.S. drones remain unverified.   • Israel politics and the war. Israel's UN ambassador Danny Danon said in an i24NEWS interview that the decision to attack Iran was America's, with Israel in support (X news summary). Russia–Ukraine

  • Komyshuvakha toll at 42. Police say 42 bodies have been recovered from the Nov 2 Russian strike on a school used as an aid point in Zaporizhzhia region, with the search continuing; it is already among the deadliest single attacks of the war (Reuters, 6:20 AM ET; Kyiv Independent).
  • Ryazan refinery hit. A Russian oil refinery in Ryazan, a large Rosneft plant, was reportedly targeted in a drone attack overnight; local officials described fires from debris and at least one injury (Kyiv Independent, 10:38 PM ET Wed; X news summary). Summaries call it the fifth major Russian refinery hit in about a week.
  • Overnight drones. Russian drone attacks injured four people in Dnipropetrovsk and Kyiv oblasts and sparked fires at infrastructure, including logistics sites in Kyiv Oblast (Kyiv Independent, 1:05 AM ET). Separately, Kyiv still faces severe power and water risk after recent energy strikes (X news summary), and chronic-illness patients are asking strangers online to mail scarce medicines after strikes on pharma logistics (Kyiv Independent).
  • EU cash. The EU disbursed over €4 billion in budget support to Ukraine (reports put the package around €4.36 billion including defense elements) (Kyiv Independent, 5:56 AM ET; X news summary).
  • Diplomacy. Russia's Kirill Dmitriev is in Washington for the first U.S. contact since Trump's midterm setback (Reuters, 5:35 AM ET). Working-level Ukraine–U.S.–Russia talks remain set for Nov 10–11 in Abu Dhabi. Zelenskyy said in a ZDF interview he hopes a Democratic Congress will strengthen military and financial support (X news summary).
  • Ukrainian loss claims. Ukraine's General Staff estimated about 2,380 Russian personnel killed or wounded in the prior day—among its highest single-day figures; these are Ukrainian estimates without independent verification (Kyiv Independent; X news summary).
