World News Brief: Nov 22, PM

Covering roughly 6:30 AM to 6:30 PM ET, Sunday, Nov 22, 2026. All times ET. Sources are posts on X from the outlets named.

Iran and Israel–Iran

  • Trump says he may restart the war in the new year. President Trump told Fox News he is "inclined" to resume strikes on Iran after the new year if there is no deal. He also warned that Iranian ships trying to run the US naval blockade would be sunk (Iran International, 12:59 PM; Al Jazeera, 1:21 PM).
  • Iranian strikes on Bahrain. Bahrain said two people were wounded in an Iranian strike Saturday on "a civilian industrial site." The Gulf kingdom has been attacked several times this week (AFP, 3:15 PM). In a Truth Social post, Trump said Iran fired more missiles and drones over the past week than at any time in more than a year, and that all were intercepted without damage (Iran International, 5:04 PM). The US State Department told Americans in Bahrain to be extra careful because of threats from Iran and the Houthis (Iran International, 5:35 PM).
  • Unconfirmed: talk of joint US–Israeli strikes. According to an X news summary, CBS News and the Financial Times cite sources saying US and Israeli forces are getting ready for possible joint strikes in the coming days or before the end of the year. No government has confirmed this.
  • Threats from Tehran. The office of Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei posted an image of a menorah made of dynamite, with a warning that Israel will cease to exist (Iran International, 3:58 PM). A senior Iranian lawmaker called for Iran to build a nuclear bomb (Iran International, 11:15 AM).
  • Hardship and unrest inside Iran. Iran began rolling power cuts because of a fuel crisis (Fox's Trey Yingst, 11:36 AM). It also banned exports of tomatoes, potatoes and onions after prices jumped (Iran International, 3:29 PM). Protests that started Nov. 17 in Abdanan, Ilam province, after a bus crash killed 13 oil and gas workers, are drawing support elsewhere, including a poster put up in southern Tehran (Iran International, 6:19 PM).
  • Iran's casualty claim. Iranian officials say 623 children were among nearly 7,200 people killed in the 2025 and 2026 wars with the US and Israel. This is Iran's own figure and has not been independently verified (Al Jazeera).

Russia–Ukraine

  • Russian missiles landed in Poland. Polish prosecutors say three Russian missiles flew into Poland Friday (Reuters, 4:45 PM). Debris from Kh-101 cruise missiles was found at three sites in eastern Poland: Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik and Mołodycz (Kyiv Independent, 6:28 PM).
  • Russian ship hit near Finland. Finnish authorities fought a fire on a sanctioned Russian cargo ship after a drone attack. Finland's prime minister blamed Ukraine for the attack (FT, 9:30 AM).
  • Strikes on Ukrainian civilians. A Russian attack on a supermarket killed 2 and injured at least 8 (Kyiv Independent, 1:56 PM). A 7-year-old boy was pulled alive from rubble hours after another strike (BBC, 5:27 PM). Russian attacks across Ukraine injured 30 over the past day (Kyiv Independent, 5:42 AM).
  • Patriot missiles. Zelensky called US delays on Patriot missiles "difficult to understand." He said Russia fired 165 missiles in a week, and he urged Washington to approve the transfer of missiles that allies have already paid for (Kyiv Independent, 5:57 AM).

Other conflicts and world news

  • Sudan. Drones hit Khartoum early Sunday. They damaged a UN facility and the area around the airport, and reports say at least four people were killed. The UN coordinator, Denise Brown, condemned the attack (UN OCHA Sudan, 3:06 AM). A Sudanese army source claimed the drones were launched from Ethiopia. This is unconfirmed (Al Arabiya, 3:05 AM).
  • West Bank. Israeli settlers raided several Palestinian towns overnight and burned about a dozen vehicles and a number of homes. Yashar chairman Gadi Eisenkot called the raids "Jewish terrorism" (Times of Israel, 5:00 PM).
  • Lebanon and Gaza. Israeli forces carried out controlled demolitions near Qantara in southern Lebanon, according to Lebanon's state news agency (Al Jazeera, 3:58 PM). In central Gaza, an Israeli drone strike killed one Palestinian, according to hospital sources (Al Jazeera, 10:05 AM).
  • Region. CENTCOM commander Adm. Brad Cooper finished a weeklong trip to Israel, Qatar and Saudi Arabia (Iran International, 4:12 PM). Pakistan's prime minister is expected in Tehran on Dec. 3 for the ECO summit (Iran International, 6:30 PM).
