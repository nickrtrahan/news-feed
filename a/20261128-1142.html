World News Brief: Nov 28, AM

Iran and Israel–Iran

U.S. blockade. The U.S. military said it disabled a Palau-flagged merchant ship in the Gulf of Oman after it left an Iranian port and ignored warnings to stop; the crew was unharmed. Tehran vowed to confront the blockade (Reuters, 9:10 PM ET Fri; Al Jazeera). The IMO's secretary-general told Al Jazeera his agency has no information about any reported Iran–Oman deal to regulate ship traffic in the Strait of Hormuz (Al Jazeera, 1:41 AM ET).

Italians killed in Kuwait. Kuwait and Italy confirmed that shrapnel from intercepted Iranian drones killed two Italian technicians at an air defense site in Kuwait on Nov 26 (ISW). Italy's foreign minister said the drone was Iranian-made; Iran denies launching it and offered to join a joint investigation (Al Jazeera; ISW).

Israeli strike planning (unconfirmed). Israeli media (Ynet/Yedioth Ahronoth) report Netanyahu is advancing plans to hit Iran's civilian infrastructure — water, power, and energy sites — to slow missile and nuclear rebuilding. The plans are reportedly still under discussion, and some officials warn of the risks (X News summary of Israeli media reports).

Washington. In video calls with troops from Mar-a-Lago, Trump said the U.S. is offering Tehran a deal to end the nine-month war (X News summary). Turkey extended its Iran gas import deal through 2032 at about 10 billion cubic meters a year, despite U.S. pressure (X News summary).

Human rights. Rights monitors and activists are highlighting death sentences handed to people arrested in January's protests. Online claims that some young women were executed this week are unverified, and Fars has denied at least one (X News summary).

Russia–Ukraine

Strikes. Russian attacks killed 10 and injured 55 across Ukraine over the past day, including a deadly strike on businesses in Chernihiv Oblast; Russia launched 162 drones overnight (Kyiv Independent, 4:34 AM ET). Russia says it shot down 1,246 Ukrainian drones overnight (Russian claim, Reuters).

Russian fuel crunch. Ukrainian strikes on refineries have cut Russian crude processing sharply since late October, and some regions are limiting fuel sales (X News summary; figures not independently verified). ISW says Moscow is moving to criminalize sharing non-approved information about strikes inside Russia (ISW).

Diplomacy. Putin claimed an understanding with the U.S. exists to end the war and blamed EU politicians for blocking it (X News summary). Jared Kushner drew criticism in Ukraine and Europe for calling Putin a "grandmaster of politics" (Kyiv Post). Ukraine proposed a limited Black Sea truce to protect grain exports, passed through India, Türkiye, and Egypt (X News summary).

Libya fallout. A Russian flotilla bound for Libya reportedly turned back after Ukraine sank a Russian warship at Benghazi on Nov 22 (Kyiv Post). Zelensky told Meloni the strike was not coordinated with European partners (Kyiv Post).

Losses. Ukraine's General Staff reported 2,825 Russian casualties in the past day (Ukrainian claim, X News summary).

Other conflicts

Gaza. MSF says an Israeli strike on its Gaza City clinic killed two staff, bringing its staff death toll to 17. Israel says the two were Hamas members but has not provided evidence (BBC; Al Jazeera). Hamas called for global protests on Sunday (Al Jazeera).

Yemen. An armed group boarded and seized a cargo ship off Yemen, UKMTO says (Reuters, 3:50 AM ET). Government-aligned and Saudi air strikes hit Houthi-held areas; Al Jazeera reports at least 121 Houthis killed or injured over the past day (Al Jazeera).

Lebanon. UN peacekeepers are preparing to leave while Israeli forces remain in the south, leaving the region's future uncertain (Times of Israel).

Other world news

  • Afghanistan: An explosion at a school killed seven students and injured nearly 100, an official says (BBC, 5:16 AM ET; Reuters).
  • Taiwan: Votes are being counted in local elections, including a close race for Taipei mayor (AFP, 5:38 AM ET).
  • U.S.: United Fidelity Bank was shut down, the biggest U.S. bank failure in two years; First Citizens bought its assets (Reuters). The military denied reports that the Air Force's cloud system was hacked (AFP).

Covers roughly 6:40 PM Fri Nov 27 to 6:45 AM ET Sat Nov 28. Claims from Iranian, Russian, and Ukrainian officials, Israeli media leaks, and X News summaries are labeled unconfirmed unless independently verified.
