World News Brief: Nov 9, AM

Top line

Trump meets Qatar’s prime minister, the main US–Iran mediator, at the White House today, though Reuters reports no deal to end the war looks imminent. Overnight Trump said renewed strikes were “highly unlikely” if talks progress, and Iranian officials said Sunday night’s US strike on Qeshm Island hit nothing vital. Iran says IAEA inspectors visited the Bushehr nuclear plant but insists it won’t ship enriched uranium abroad, and President Pezeshkian says the supreme leader ordered an end to the “no war, no peace” limbo. In Ukraine, Russia launched 317 drones overnight as blackouts grip Kyiv, Ukrainian drones reportedly hit a major refinery near St. Petersburg, and the Kremlin disputes that Europe will take part in this week’s Abu Dhabi talks. Covers roughly 7 PM Sunday to 7 AM ET Monday, Nov. 9.

Iran and Israel–Iran

  • Diplomacy via Qatar. The White House said Trump meets Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani today as Doha keeps mediating (Iran International, 7:37 PM ET Sun; Reuters, 1:10 AM ET). Reuters’ latest framing is that no imminent deal is in sight (Reuters, 6:30 AM ET). Axios reported the Qatari premier is seeking more time for diplomacy (Iran International citing Axios, 10:23 PM ET Sun). An X news summary describes a weekend proposal letting Iran enrich to 3.67% at home, down-blend about 400 kg of higher-enriched material, lift the US naval blockade and free $6–10 billion in assets; those terms are unconfirmed by the outlets cited here (X news summary).
  • Trump’s remarks. Trump repeated that new strikes were “highly unlikely” if Iran signs “a very strong agreement,” adding “I would if necessary” (AFP, 2:33 AM ET; Reuters, 9:10 PM ET Sun). He told 60 Minutes the two sides were close to a deal before the midterms and suggested Democratic wins encouraged Tehran to hold out (Iran International, 10:16 PM ET Sun), said “We won in Iran. It’s just a question of closing it out now or later” (Iran International, 8:17 PM ET Sun), and said he expects to discuss ending the war with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin at next week’s APEC summit in China (Reuters, 10:45 PM ET Sun).
  • Tehran’s line. Foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said IAEA inspectors visited the Bushehr nuclear plant several days ago and Iran hasn’t suspended its NPT commitments (Iran International, 3:26 AM ET; Times of Israel, 5:13 AM ET). He also said Iran will not transfer enriched uranium out of the country (Iran International, 6:33 AM ET), that Iran wants measures against it lifted so it can meet its commitments within two days (Iran International, 4:35 AM ET), and that Iran sees “no difference” between US parties (Iran International, 2:53 AM ET). President Pezeshkian said the supreme leader had ordered officials to end the “no war, no peace” situation and that a dignified agreement was possible (Iran International, 5:20 AM ET). In a harder note, lawmaker Hamidreza Gudarzi argued Iran should pursue nuclear weapons as a deterrent; he is one legislator, not official policy (Iran International, 5:43 AM ET). Araghchi accused Israel of dragging successive US administrations into wars (Iran International, 11:22 PM ET Sun).
  • Qeshm strike aftermath. A Hormozgan deputy governor told state media Sunday night’s US attack on Qeshm Island hit sites outside residential and commercial areas and damaged no vital infrastructure or services (Iran International, 12:14 AM ET). The US has not publicly detailed the strike in the reporting reviewed here.
  • Hormuz. Iran said no unauthorized vessels transited the strait overnight after the IRGC Navy destroyed what it called enemy navigation aids in the southern channel; this is Iran’s own claim, not independently verified (Reuters, 3:35 AM ET). An IRGC spokesman called claims that mines had been cleared from the strait “an outright lie” (Iran International, 1:38 AM ET). Iran International, citing a confidential document, reported Iran is using terminals and storage in China to keep sanctioned tankers running (Iran International, 9:48 PM ET Sun).
  • Region and markets. UAE adviser Anwar Gargash warned of prolonged regional uncertainty and said a full US–Iran settlement looks difficult (Reuters, 2:10 AM ET). Turkey’s Erdogan said he told Trump he doesn’t want a new wave of attacks on Iran (Iran International, 5:36 AM ET). China’s Middle East envoy Zhai Jun met Iranian security official Ali Bagheri Kani in Iran (Iran International, 2:05 AM ET). Brent crude dipped to about $97.67 at the open and Gulf stocks rose on Trump’s comments (Iran International, 8:28 PM ET Sun; Reuters, 4:40 AM ET).
  • Iraq (unverified). An X news summary says Iraqi PM Ali al-Zaidi described to Shiite leaders a leaked US war plan involving scenarios of ground forces crossing Iraq; it rests on anonymous sources with no official confirmation (X news summary). The same feed says the PM condemned the Khor Mor gas field drone attack and opened a probe (X news summary).

Russia–Ukraine

  • Overnight attack. Russia launched 317 long-range drones, including 105 jet-powered ones, plus cruise missiles, and attacks injured 28 people across Ukraine over the past day as strikes on energy continue (Kyiv Independent, 6:55 AM ET).
  • Blackouts. Zelenskyy said he and Ursula von der Leyen agreed to activate an EU emergency mechanism for fast repair funding, with power, water and heat hardest hit in Kyiv, Dnipro, Sumy and other regions (Zelenskyy, 2:58 AM ET). AFP and the Kyiv Independent describe Kyiv residents walking darkened streets and stocking up ahead of winter (AFP, 11:46 PM ET Sun; Kyiv Independent, 5:27 AM ET). ISW says Russia is using its winter strike campaign to degrade Ukraine’s energy security (ISW, 9:00 PM ET Sun).
  • Ukrainian strikes. Ukrainian drones reportedly hit the Kirishi (Kinef) refinery, Russia’s second-largest, about 500 miles north of Ukraine, causing large explosions (Kyiv Independent, 11:07 PM ET Sun).
  • Abu Dhabi talks, Nov. 10–11. A senior EU official told the Kyiv Independent the EU, NATO and the national security advisers of Germany, France and the UK will take part (Kyiv Independent, 4:23 AM ET). Kremlin spokesman Peskov countered that the talks are trilateral between Russia and Ukraine with US mediation (Kyiv Independent, 6:41 AM ET). Trump told 60 Minutes he thinks a deal is close and that Ukraine will likely “end up with a little bit different shape” (Kyiv Independent, 9:58 PM ET Sun). G7 foreign ministers meet in Paris today (Reuters, 10:50 PM ET Sun).
  • Losses. Ukraine’s General Staff reported record Russian losses for Nov. 8 (Kyiv Independent, 5:39 AM ET); an X summary puts it at 2,980 in a day, a Ukrainian figure not independently verified (X news summary). ISW, citing analyses of data hackers say they took from Russia’s statistics agency, said Russian deaths may be near 450,000 and likely higher; Russia denies a breach (ISW, 9:45 PM ET Sun).
  • Human story. Reuters profiled an elderly couple reunited after the husband spent four and a half years trapped in Russian-held Oleshky (Reuters, 6:10 AM ET).

Other conflicts

Other world news

  • Senegal. The migrant boat death toll rose to 39 as more bodies were found, emergency services said (AP, 6:53 AM ET).
  • Poland. The Krakow gunman died in hospital, and prosecutors say there’s no evidence yet tying him to any ideology (Reuters, 5:54 AM ET; AFP, 6:50 AM ET).
  • Climate. COP31 opened in Antalya, Turkey, with an early fight over money as the UN chief urged leaders to “get serious” (AFP, 6:20 AM ET).
  • France. An EU report says a covert Russian network exploited French riots on social media (Reuters exclusive, 5:45 AM ET), as PM Lecornu holds talks on protesters’ cost-of-living concerns (Reuters, 5:50 AM ET).
  • Nvidia. CEO Jensen Huang acknowledged an affair with a woman US officials say worked for Chinese intelligence, which Beijing denies; Nvidia shares fell as its internal probe continues (AFP, 5:50 AM ET; Reuters, 6:09 AM ET).
  • New Zealand and the Philippines. PM Christopher Luxon looks set to keep power with a razor-thin coalition majority (Reuters, 6:20 AM ET), and lawyers for Vice President Sara Duterte opened her defense at trial (Reuters, 6:30 AM ET).
