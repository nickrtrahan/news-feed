World News Brief: Nov 27, PM

Iran and Israel–Iran

U.S. disables a ship trying to run the Iran blockade. The U.S. military said it disabled a bulk carrier that left Bandar Abbas and tried to break the naval blockade. It is the first ship the U.S. has reported disabling since Tehran vowed to break the blockade (Iran International, 2:49 PM ET; Reuters, 5:00 PM ET).

Tehran stays defiant on Hormuz. Mohammad Mokhber, an aide to the supreme leader, told state media the Strait of Hormuz will stay closed and the blockade on Iran will be lifted (Iran International, 1:03 PM ET). Reuters reports Iran is also warning about foreign military forces in the Gulf as South Korea readies a bigger naval role (Reuters).

Kuwait blames Iran for deadly drone attack. Kuwait summoned Iran's ambassador over a Thursday drone attack on what it called a "vital site," which killed two Italian nationals and wounded a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani (Iran International, 9:47 AM ET). Italy's foreign minister Antonio Tajani said Kuwaiti investigators found the drone was made in Iran; Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi denied it (Iran International, 2:05 PM ET).

Israel reportedly weighing strikes on Iran's civilian infrastructure (unconfirmed). An X News summary of Israeli reports says Netanyahu is considering hitting Iran's water, power, and oil infrastructure, with Mossad and IDF leaders split on whether to wait for the U.S. This is not confirmed by an official source (X News summary).

Diplomacy. Turkey's foreign minister Hakan Fidan said Turkey, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan discussed prospects for a good outcome in U.S.–Iran talks at a meeting in Islamabad (Iran International, 5:23 PM ET). Turkey also extended its gas import deal with Iran to 2032, per Bloomberg (Iran International).

Inside Iran. A truckers' strike over fuel shortages and low freight rates hit its sixth day in 20 cities, the union says (Iran International). A court sentenced 21-year-old protester Taraneh Rahimi to death and her twin Romina to 25 years; the family alleges torture, which Iran denies, and the verdicts can be appealed (X News summary, citing BBC Persian). At least 26 people have died in a wave of bootleg alcohol poisonings (Iran International).

Ripple effects. Norse Atlantic filed for bankruptcy and halted flights as the Iran war hits airlines (Reuters).

Russia–Ukraine

Ukraine hits missile fuel plant in Tula. Ukraine's General Staff confirmed a strike on a ballistic missile fuel production plant in Russia's Tula Oblast, carried out with a resistance group inside Russia (Kyiv Independent, 10:37 AM ET). Zelensky said Ukraine hit oil and military sites in 11 Russian regions this past week (Kyiv Independent).

Grid under strain. Ukraine stopped exporting electricity as Russian attacks strain its power grid (Kyiv Independent). ISW notes Russia is exploiting Ukraine's shortage of Patriot interceptors (ISW).

Talks and politics. Trump said he asked Putin to "pull back all military aggression" against Europe, a shift in tone (AFP, 6:40 AM ET). ISW says Moscow keeps demanding Ukraine leave the rest of Donbas before any ceasefire (ISW). Zelensky said nine children were brought back from Russian-held areas through Melania Trump's mediation (Kyiv Independent).

Europe. Germany approved nearly €12 billion in Ukraine support for 2027 (Kyiv Independent), but an EU summit failed to make real progress on Ukraine funding (Kyiv Independent). Estonia will close all border crossings with Russia on Dec. 1 (Reuters), and a drone briefly entered Latvian airspace (Reuters). Zelensky admitted Ukraine did not coordinate its Nov. 22 naval-drone strike on a Russian corvette in Libya with European partners (Kyiv Independent).

Gaza and the Middle East

Two MSF guards killed in Gaza City strike. MSF said an Israeli strike on one of its clinics killed two of its watchmen Friday night, bringing its staff deaths in Gaza to 17 (MSF, 5:04 PM ET). Israel had not publicly commented in sources reviewed; some pro-Israel accounts claim the men were Hamas members (unconfirmed).

Other strikes. Local journalists and Gaza Civil Defense reported a deadly Israeli strike in Rafah (X News summary). Hamas says Israel targeted the son of senior leader Khalil al-Hayya, who survived (Hamas claim, X News summary).

Yemen. Saudi Arabia said it destroyed a Houthi drone aimed at the Riyadh area, disrupting some flights (X News summary).

Other world news

  • Hunger: UN food agencies warn hunger will worsen in 16 hotspots through May 2027, with Sudan and Yemen among the worst (AFP; Reuters).
  • Spain: Campaigning ended for Sunday's snap election, which is expected to favor conservatives and the far right; Sánchez urged voters not to go "backwards" (AFP, 6:00 PM ET). A tanker carrying diesel capsized off Ibiza, killing one (AFP).
  • Iraq: The prime minister is cracking down on Iran-backed militias and ordered a takeover of Kurdish-run oil fields (Reuters).

Covers roughly 6:45 AM to 6:40 PM ET Fri Nov 27. Claims from Iranian state media, Hamas, and X News summaries are labeled unconfirmed unless independently verified.
