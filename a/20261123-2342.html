World News Brief: Nov 23, PM

Iran and Israel–Iran

Trump floats strikes after New Year. President Trump told Fox News he leans toward resuming military strikes on Iran after January 1 if no deal is reached, called Iran's negotiators "professional tap dancers," and warned that vessels challenging the U.S. naval blockade near the Strait of Hormuz would be sunk (X news summary of the interview; related reporting). Iran's Foreign Ministry called the blockade an act of military aggression and vowed to counter it "by all available means." IRGC commander Ahmad Vahidi warned the Guards would respond to any attack "wherever" it comes from (Iran International).

New U.S. sanctions on Iran's shadow banking. On Monday the Treasury sanctioned 22 companies and eight individuals tied to Iranian currency-exchange houses and trading fronts in Iran, the UAE, Turkey and Hong Kong (Iran International). The State Department said Iran uses the network to buy weapons and fund proxies (Iran International). A U.S. official cited by Al Arabiya said 108 million barrels of oil moved through Hormuz in one week and dared Tehran to test the blockade (Iran International).

Diplomacy keeps going. Qatar's prime minister met Vice President JD Vance on Monday about the Iran talks, Axios reported (Iran International). He also warned that the region "cannot withstand" another U.S.–Iran round (Iran International). Iran's foreign minister said Tehran will keep negotiating for sanctions relief while building up its resilience (Iran International). Iran is also seeking $290 billion in damages from the U.S. (Times of Israel).

Congress and the war's funding. Key Senate Republicans are considering a deal with Democrats to cut off funding for the Iran war while keeping Pentagon funding, ahead of the December 11 shutdown deadline, according to a Washington Post report (via Iran International; Al Jazeera).

The Israeli debate. A former Mossad chief said Israel must go to war with Iran again "as soon as all conditions allow" (Times of Israel). Gadi Eisenkot said after a security briefing that Israel faces no immediate Iranian threat and should focus on removing Iran's enriched uranium (Times of Israel). Caretaker PM Netanyahu warned against downplaying the threats (Al Jazeera), and his coalition talks with Eisenkot's party collapsed. Reports that Israel is weighing a unilateral strike if Washington delays are unconfirmed.

Inside Iran. President Pezeshkian went on state TV to urge people to conserve electricity and water amid shortages. Iran's intelligence ministry said it broke up four armed separatist cells in West Azerbaijan province and arrested 27 people (these are regime claims, not independently verified). Singer Parastoo Ahmadi and four colleagues were reportedly flogged in Qom over a hijab-free online concert (X news summary). Retired Gen. Jack Keane said the CIA and Mossad are arming Iranian opposition groups. That claim is unconfirmed.

Russia–Ukraine

Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya. President Zelensky said Ukrainian sea drones sank a Russian navy corvette at a port in Libya. He released SBU video he said shows the attack (Reuters; AP). AFP reports that Ukraine claims weekend strikes on two Russian vessels near Benghazi (AFP; BBC analysis).

Zelensky at NATO's Parliamentary Assembly. Zelensky asked allies for concrete Article 5-style security guarantees and more Patriot missiles. He warned that Russia is preparing new strikes (Kyiv Post). NATO's Mark Rutte said Russia's war machine is "running at full speed" (Kyiv Post). Zelensky also said Russia is deliberately hitting repaired power lines in Odesa region (Kyiv Post).

Peace push and Putin's terms. The Trump administration wants a Ukraine–Russia deal by early January (X news summary). Pressed by the BBC's Steve Rosenberg after the APEC summit, Putin said Russia would end the war only if Ukraine gives up the rest of Donbas (Rosenberg exchange).

Casualties and escalation. Russian attacks killed 4 people, including a 15-year-old boy, and injured 19 across Ukraine over the past day (Kyiv Independent). Dmitry Medvedev wrote that international law permits Russia to use nuclear weapons for its security (X news summary). A German media investigation claims Russia is preparing to put a nuclear weapon in space. That claim is unconfirmed (Kyiv Independent). The EU has proposed a 22nd sanctions package (Kyiv Independent).

Russia–Estonia tension. Moscow accused Estonia of hostile actions on the "verge of triggering open war" (Reuters).

Other Conflicts

Gaza. Israeli strikes killed at least three people in Gaza, including a worker for the UN Palestinian agency, despite the ceasefire in place since October 2025 (Reuters). An Oxfam report says contamination from the war now covers more than 99% of Gaza (BBC). President Herzog condemned Jewish extremist attacks on Palestinians in the West Bank as "despicable" (Times of Israel). Jared Kushner warned that Israel risks "global-pariah status" (Times of Israel).

Sudan. Drone strikes hit homes and a UN building in Khartoum and killed at least four civilians, including children. Sudanese military sources blamed the RSF, but that attribution is unconfirmed. Several Gulf states, Egypt and the U.S. condemned the strikes (X news summary).

Korean Peninsula. South Korea says a suspected North Korean aircraft breached its airspace near a small eastern island and told residents to take shelter (AP). North Korea also threatened to close the Swiss diplomatic office (AFP).

Yemen. The Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted a Houthi drone launched toward southern Saudi Arabia (Iran International).
