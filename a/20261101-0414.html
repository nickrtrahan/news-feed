Iran: Khamenei orders a response with no geographic limits

Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei reportedly told top commanders to update target lists covering US and Israeli assets worldwide and to prepare an immediate response, with no geographic limits, if either country attacks again. Fox News and Al Jazeera are cited. (X story, updated 11:51 PM ET; related)

Iran would reduce its enriched uranium stockpile and reopen the Strait of Hormuz, and the US would give sanctions relief. There is no final deal. Secretary of State Rubio warned at the G20 in Atlanta about Iranian threats to shipping. (X story)

Russia–Ukraine: Moscow invites Zelensky and refinery strikes resume

At the G20, Lavrov said Zelensky can come to Moscow for direct talks with Putin and that his security would be guaranteed. Russia rejects neutral venues. Ukrainian reaction is skeptical, and US-involved talks in Abu Dhabi are expected. (X story)

  • On Oct 30, Ukraine resumed drone strikes on Russian refineries, including Novokuibyshevsk in Samara, after a pause of about two weeks. Russian officials report minor damage. (X story)
