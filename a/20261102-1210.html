World News Brief: Nov 2, AM

Covering roughly 7 PM Sunday to 7 AM Monday ET. Items marked "unconfirmed" come from claims or X story summaries that haven't been independently verified.

Iran and Israel–Iran

Talks in Doha, threats on both sides. Oil fell more than 3% Monday morning, with Brent dipping below $114 a barrel, on reports of indirect US–Iran talks in Doha. Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said Tehran won't move its 60% enriched uranium stockpile and wants the US naval blockade ended and sanctions relief before any deal; officials describe a draft proposal circulating and possible follow-up talks (X news summary).

At a rally in Dubuque, Iowa, Sunday night, President Trump said the US has already damaged Iran's capabilities and that the conflict would end "very soon, one way or another." Iran warned that any renewed war could last longer than the previous round, after Trump said he still had to "take care" of more targets (AFP). Unconfirmed: reports circulating on X describe discussions of post-election strikes and timing talks with Netanyahu (X news summary).

Gulf on edge. Gulf diplomats, per Bloomberg and Financial Times reporting summarized on X, fear fresh US–Israeli strikes on a better-prepared Iran could bring heavier retaliation against energy sites and Hormuz shipping (X news summary).

Tankers hit. UKMTO reported a tanker struck by two projectiles about 72 nautical miles east of Ras al-Khafji, Saudi Arabia, on Nov. 1, crew safe; a separate Mayday came from the Strait of Hormuz after another vessel was hit. Iran's IRGC Navy claimed the ships hit its naval mines on an "unauthorized route" (X news summary). Qatar also condemned an attack Friday on its LNG carrier Al Mayeda in the Gulf; all 25 crew were unharmed (X news summary).

Diplomacy. China's Wang Yi met Iranian security official Ali Bagheri Kani in Beijing Monday and urged dialogue; Beijing plans a special envoy trip to Iran, Oman and Saudi Arabia this month (X news summary). FM Abbas Araghchi is in Turkmenistan for a one-day visit on trade and energy (X news summary). Reza Pahlavi begins a Belgium–Germany trip Nov. 3 and will accept the Friedrich Naumann Foundation's Freedom Prize in Frankfurt on Nov. 7 (X news summary).

Israel

Post-election coalition maneuvering continues. The Times of Israel reports the anti-Netanyahu bloc won a combined 67 seats but faces a hard path to forming a government (Times of Israel). Avigdor Liberman says he'll recommend Gadi Eisenkot for prime minister and Yair Lapid for Knesset speaker (Times of Israel).

In Gaza, Israeli strikes killed at least three Palestinians Monday, per Gaza health officials, after eight were reported killed Sunday; Israel says it targeted militants and accuses Hamas of ceasefire violations (X news summary). Israeli forces pulled back from key Hebron neighborhoods after an 11-day operation that displaced more than 3,300 Palestinians (X news summary).

Russia–Ukraine

Massive drone barrage. Russia ended a roughly 48-hour assault late Nov. 1 that reportedly involved more than 1,400 drones aimed at substations, rail lines and airbases in 22 of 24 regions, causing blackouts in cities including Kyiv and Dnipro (X news summary; drone total unconfirmed). A Russian drone strike near a vocational school dormitory in Vinnytsia Oblast on Nov. 1 killed a 13-year-old girl and a woman and injured several children (X news summary).

Zelensky's offers. Zelensky says he's ready to meet Putin in Turkey, calling Ankara a realistic mediator (Kyiv Independent). In a Fox News interview he proposed pausing strikes on Russian refineries for a month in exchange for US Patriot missiles over that period (X news summary).

Losses and the front. Ukraine's General Staff claims a record 2,500 Russian casualties on Nov. 1 and 54,400 in October (Ukrainian figures, not independently verified). ISW has assessed Ukraine made net territorial gains in October (X news summary). Sky News drone footage reportedly shows Russian forces using a humanitarian ceasefire in occupied Oleshky, Kherson Oblast, to bring in troops and ammunition (X news summary).

Reuters published a visual investigation finding many of at least 7,000 properties listed as "ownerless" in occupied Zaporizhzhia actually have owners (Reuters). In Moscow, Novaya Gazeta says it will shut down Nov. 9 after mounting state pressure (X news summary).

Other conflicts

  • Sudan: Sudan accuses Ethiopia of letting the RSF fly drones from Assosa Airport and says new strikes hit power stations in Kassala State on Nov. 2; Ethiopia denies it, and the claims aren't independently verified (X news summary).
  • Western Sahara: The UN's 35-year MINURSO mission ended Sunday after its mandate lapsed amid a US–Russia standoff at the Security Council (Reuters; X news summary).
  • Ethiopia: Videos show unidentified armed personnel in a Tigray town near the Eritrean border amid renewed clashes; claims they are foreign mercenaries are unconfirmed (X news summary).

Top world news

  • Bulgaria elected Iliana Iotova, an ally of PM Radev, as its first female president (Reuters).
  • South Africans are voting in municipal elections (Reuters).
  • A cyber-attack hit supermarket freezers in Belgium and Sweden (BBC).
  • Riots followed a demonstration in Frankfurt on Saturday; 30 people were detained (AP).
  • US midterms are Tuesday, Nov. 3; in Michigan, Democrat Haley Stevens is defending her endorsement switch to Republican Mike Rogers (BBC).
