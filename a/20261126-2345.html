World News Brief: Nov 26, PM

Covering roughly 6:30 AM–6:45 PM ET, Thursday, Nov 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving). Sources are linked inline; claims from one side or not independently verified are labeled.

Iran and Israel–Iran

Two tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz (IRGC claim). Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said Thursday morning that two unidentified oil tankers hit naval mines and caught fire north of Oman's Khasab port, one of them a supertanker carrying more than 2 million barrels of crude. The IRGC said the ships had their tracking systems off and were using an "unauthorized" route with U.S. air cover (Iran International, ~5:20 AM ET; Al Jazeera). Unconfirmed: the ships' identities, the damage and the IRGC's account of U.S. involvement have not been independently verified. The same day, the IRGC navy commander claimed "full control" over both approaches to the strait (Iran International).

Hormuz talks. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi told state TV that Qatari and Pakistani mediators are still trading proposals, but said Iran's demands must be met before the strait can reopen (Iran International, ~4:15 PM ET). South Korea told Tehran it will stay out of the U.S.–Iran war while expanding a naval mission south of Oman (Iran International).

Two Italians killed in a strike in Kuwait. Reuters and the BBC report that two Italian workers were killed in what a source called an Iranian strike in Kuwait (Reuters, ~1:10 PM ET; BBC). Araghchi denied Iranian involvement and offered Kuwait a joint investigation (Iran International).

Trump: "victory at hand." On Thanksgiving calls with U.S. troops, President Trump said victory over Iran "will happen whenever I want it to happen," but that Washington is giving Tehran "every chance to make a deal" (Iran International, ~6:40 PM ET). Separately, i24NEWS reported, citing former deputy national security adviser Victoria Coates, that Trump is leaning toward postponing any new strike until Israel forms a stable government (Iran International citing i24NEWS). That second report is secondhand and unconfirmed by the White House.

Israel says it is ready to act alone. IDF spokesman Brig. Gen. Effie Defrin said Israel is ready to strike Iran "at any given moment – even independently without the Americans" (Al Jazeera, ~5:20 AM ET). Unconfirmed: posts circulating widely quote Defense Minister Israel Katz as saying Israel shared intelligence with Washington that Iran is developing chemical weapons (example post); I could not find this reported by a major outlet in this window. Iran's IRGC, meanwhile, said its missile production is uninterrupted (PressTV, Iranian state media), and Iran's atomic energy chief called claims that Iran wants a bomb "fabricated" (Al Jazeera).

New underground complex. The Institute for Science and International Security says Iran is rapidly building a fortified underground complex at Behjatabad, between the Isfahan and Natanz nuclear sites; the institute says its purpose "is not clear at this time" (Iran International).

Inside Iran. A 21-year-old woman, Taraneh Rahimi, arrested in January protests in Isfahan, has reportedly been sentenced to death; her family alleges a confession was extracted by torture (Daily Mail; the family's account, not independently verified). Iranians also describe soaring food prices amid a collapsing rial (Iran International).

Iran's allies: Houthis vs. Saudi Arabia

An attack on Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery killed a Nepali worker, injured others and halted some operations, Saudi Arabia's energy ministry said; the Saudi-led coalition blamed the Houthis (Reuters, ~4:40 PM ET; Al Jazeera). The coalition says it then struck 50 Houthi military targets in Sanaa, Hodeidah and Saada (Iran International citing coalition spokesman). Oil prices ticked up on the escalation after falling about 20% in recent weeks (CBS News).

Russia–Ukraine

Strikes on Kyiv and Kharkiv. Russian ballistic missiles hit Kyiv overnight, wounding seven and damaging a shopping center and two other buildings (Reuters). The Kyiv Independent counts 10 killed and 58 injured across Ukraine over the past day; the Air Force reported 113 drones launched overnight (Kyiv Independent). Ukraine says its new "Blysk" laser system shot down a jet-powered Russian drone for the first time (CBS News).

Zelensky warns of a new mass strike. Zelensky said intelligence shows Russia is finishing preparations for another large attack on energy, logistics and essential services (Kyiv Independent, ~6:50 AM ET). In a BBC interview he said the U.S. is too focused on the Middle East and is not pushing Russia hard enough (BBC, ~5:45 PM ET).

Diplomacy. Trump said he urged Putin, during their talks in China, to pull back military aggression across Europe so a deal can come quickly; he briefed NATO's Mark Rutte at Mar-a-Lago (Kyiv Post; CBS News). The Kyiv Independent notes envoys Witkoff and Kushner have made 14 trips to Russia since February 2025 versus two to Ukraine (Kyiv Independent), after Kushner called Putin a "grandmaster of politics" on a podcast.

Other. Ukraine's General Staff says Russia lost 55,629 troops in Nov. 1–25, its worst month of the war (General Staff of Ukraine; Ukrainian figures, not independently verified). Italy summoned Ukraine's military attaché after Ukrainian strikes on Russian vessels near Benghazi, Libya (Kyiv Post). A Russian-installed official said a Ukrainian drone attack killed five in occupied Zaporizhzhia (Al Jazeera; Russian-side claim).

Israel–Gaza and Lebanon

Gaza's health ministry says Israeli attacks killed 19 Palestinians in the past 48 hours, bringing the toll since the October 2025 ceasefire to 1,705 (Al Jazeera). Israel has stepped up targeted killings of Hamas commanders even as Washington presses Netanyahu to carry out the U.S. ceasefire plan (CNN). Defense Minister Katz threatened to move one million Gazans south unless Hamas disarms (Al Jazeera); Hamas's leader said ongoing strikes "cannot be accepted" (Times of Israel). Lebanese state media reported Israeli strikes and shelling in southern Lebanon Thursday evening (Al Jazeera).

Other world news

  • A fuel-tanker crash and fire on a Cairo highway killed at least 31 people; the 21-year-old driver was detained (AP; Al Jazeera).
  • A diesel tanker carrying about 7,000 tonnes sank off Ibiza, killing one crew member; no pollution reported so far (Reuters).
  • EU leaders clashed over the bloc's budget at a Brussels summit, and Czech PM Andrej Babiš questioned the scale of aid to Ukraine (AFP).
  • Guinea-Bissau's military rulers delayed next month's elections by two years (Al Jazeera).
  • Iraqi Kurds protested Baghdad's moves to tighten central control (Reuters).
  • Pope Leo XIV told the Vatican's interreligious dialogue office that peace "is not merely the absence of war" (@Pontifex).
