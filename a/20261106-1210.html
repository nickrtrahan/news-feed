Iran and Israel–Iran

Iranian lawmakers on the parliament security committee warned that U.S. strikes launched from European bases could draw Iranian missiles toward Italy, Germany, and other host countries; treat the rhetoric as threat messaging, not a confirmed launch order (X news summary). Maritime firm Windward tracked Iran-linked vessels loitering over undersea cable routes in the Gulf of Oman after IRGC warnings that such links could be targeted if new U.S.–Israeli strikes begin; no sabotage has been confirmed (X news summary).
  • Oil and Hormuz. Brent climbed back toward the $100 area Friday as traders priced renewed escalation risk after midterm-driven swings; X news summaries put prices near about $99.90 after an earlier selloff (X news summary). Earlier Iranian claims of tanker hits and drone shootdowns near Hormuz remain without U.S. confirmation (X news summary).
  • Carrier and Iraq. Open-source trackers say the USS Theodore Roosevelt is stopping in Singapore en route toward Central Command, with arrival in the theater not expected before mid-November; that timing is not an official strike schedule (X news summary). Iraq’s civil aviation authority kept airspace limits for military activity Nov 7–11 over major airports and activated nuclear-emergency monitoring along the Iran border, citing low fallout risk (X news summary). Sirens at Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan on Nov 5 were later assessed as an early-warning test, not an incoming attack (X news summary).

In Israel’s government-formation talks, opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot led Netanyahu in Knesset recommendations (about 67 to 46) ahead of President Herzog’s mandate decision; coalition arithmetic remains unsettled (X news summary).

Iran’s Revolutionary Court sentenced activist Amirhossein Mousavi to death for “corruption on earth” tied to posts on X, plus a separate prison term for insulting religious sanctities; family and rights advocates say a televised confession was coerced and are seeking international pressure to halt the execution (X news summary).

Russia–Ukraine

  • Komyshuvakha at 53. Kyiv Independent reports the death toll from the Nov 2 Russian strike on a school used as an aid point in Komyshuvakha, Zaporizhzhia Oblast, has risen to 53 as the search enters a fifth day—the deadliest Russian attack on that oblast since the full-scale invasion began. Russia says it hit military targets; Ukraine and the UN emphasize the civilian aid gathering (Kyiv Independent, 6:10 AM ET; X news summary).
  • Blackouts and energy war. Overnight Russian drones and strikes hit energy sites, with outages reported across Kyiv and Vinnytsia, Dnipropetrovsk, Zhytomyr, Zaporizhzhia, Kharkiv, and Chernihiv regions; some areas faced up to about 19–20 hours without power as winter cold set in (X news summary; related). Ukrainian drones overnight struck the Afipsky oil refinery in Krasnodar, with large fires reported; Russian authorities blamed falling debris and claimed dozens of drones downed. The hit follows recent strikes on Ryazan and other Russian refining sites (Kyiv Independent, 11:47 PM ET Nov 5; X news summary; ISW).
  • Diplomacy track. Kyiv Independent reports Trump met Russian envoy Kirill Dmitriev in Washington and claimed a “big, beautiful agreement” is coming ahead of U.S.–Ukraine–Russia working-level talks. Jared Kushner described a U.S. plan for security guarantees and a broader European framework, with trilateral sessions in Abu Dhabi next week after Brussels consultations—details remain unconfirmed and no deal text is public (Kyiv Independent, 9:57 PM ET Nov 5; X news summary). Melania Trump’s White House event marking the Oleshky local ceasefire and evacuation of nearly 800 civilians continued to draw coverage overnight (Kyiv Independent; X news summary).
  • Aid and politics. Zelenskyy said Ukraine has “increased chances” of more U.S. aid after Democrats’ midterm wins and still needs to consolidate bipartisan support; he also thanked Spain for about $490 million focused on air defenses (Kyiv Independent, 7:18 PM ET Nov 5; Spain aid). An X news summary says the EU disbursed about €4.36 billion under its multi-year loan program for budget and defense needs (X news summary). Ukraine’s General Staff put cumulative Russian personnel losses above 1.6 million; those are Ukrainian estimates and are not independently verified (Kyiv Independent, 2:24 AM ET; X news summary).
  • UK and sport. The UK announced a Ukrainian-language GCSE from 2028 for students in England, Wales, and Northern Ireland, aimed in part at refugee children (X news summary). The IIHF continues to resist a CAS ruling that would restore Russian teams, while Canada still plans to deny visas for the World Juniors in Alberta (X news summary).
