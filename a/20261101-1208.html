World News Brief: Nov 1, AM

Covering roughly 7 PM ET Saturday, Oct 31 through 7 AM ET Sunday, Nov 1. All times are Eastern. Items drawn from X story summaries (compiled from posts, not wire copy) are marked as such; treat them as less certain than direct outlet reports.

Iran and Israel–Iran

Trump says one or two Iranian targets remain. President Trump said Saturday the US has knocked out much of Iran's military but still needs to "take care of" one or two sites, adding that talks are "going fine" but Tehran is "not very reliable" (Times of Israel, 2:03 AM; AFP, 3:40 AM).

Tehran warns of a longer war. Iran's army said any new war could be "longer than the previous conflict" and that Trump won't dictate its duration (Times of Israel, 5:19 AM). Per an X story summary, senior officials including Mohsen Rezaei vowed retaliation against US forces "wherever reachable," and Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei reportedly ordered the military to prepare unrestricted retaliation (X story).

Iran designates the Royal Navy and RAF as terrorist organizations. According to an X story summary, Iran's Foreign Ministry on Sunday labeled the UK Royal Navy and Royal Air Force terrorist groups and MI6 a hostile entity, citing reciprocity for Britain's measures against the IRGC and UK base access for US and Israeli operations (X story, ~6 AM). Not yet confirmed by a major wire in this window.

Strait of Hormuz tanker incidents. An X story summary reports the IRGC claimed four tankers hit naval mines on "unauthorized routes," plus separate reports of a projectile strike on the Qatari-owned LNG tanker Al Mayeda and limpet mines on the Panama-flagged Louise 1. Oman evacuated 25 crew from a burning tanker; no deaths were reported, and US Central Command said main shipping lanes remain clear (X story, updated 5:20 PM Sat). Details remain unconfirmed.

Rubio at the G20. Secretary of State Marco Rubio accused Iran of threatening to blow up ships and regional energy infrastructure; the G20 foreign ministers' meeting in Atlanta reportedly ended without a joint communique over divisions on Iran and Ukraine (X story).

  • Iran's army spokesman said Iran has completed purchase of Russian Su-35 fighters, with delivery awaiting the end of the war (X story).
  • A 5.0 earthquake struck about 115 km southeast of Bushehr at 4:20 PM ET Saturday, per USGS data cited in an X summary; no damage to the nuclear plant reported, and claims of a weapons test are unsupported (X story).
  • A viral claim that Pakistan's ambassador was assaulted in Tehran is false; the video shows an Armenian parliament brawl (X story).
  • Iranian security chief Rezaei claimed on state TV that Saudi Arabia's defense minister warned Israel could target Turkey and Saudi Arabia next; Riyadh, Ankara and Islamabad have not confirmed (X story). Unconfirmed.

Israel: Election Deadlock

Netanyahu's coalition fell to 53 Knesset seats in the Oct 27 election, short of a majority; another election still looks most likely unless opposition figures break campaign promises (Times of Israel, 5:00 AM). President Herzog urged party leaders to "face the reality" of the results and avoid another vote (Times of Israel, 4:58 AM). Tens of thousands marched in Tel Aviv Saturday calling for a Jewish–Arab government to replace Netanyahu (X story). Ultra-Orthodox protesters blocked roads near the IDF's Tel Hashomer recruitment office Sunday morning over the draft (Times of Israel, 4:06 AM).

Russia–Ukraine

Record Russian losses. Ukraine's General Staff says Russia lost an estimated 54,400 troops killed or wounded in October, its worst month of the full-scale war (Kyiv Independent, 5:01 AM). These are Ukrainian figures and can't be independently verified.

Refinery strikes resume. Ukrainian drones hit Lukoil's Kstovo (NORSI) refinery in Nizhny Novgorod, reportedly the third strike in three days; the regional governor said over 100 drones were downed (X story, 5:16 AM). Damage extent is unconfirmed. Ukraine restarted refinery strikes Oct 30 after a two-week pause (X story).

Energy war. Russian missile and drone attacks on Oct 30 cut power to about 200,000 households in Dnipro and over 80,000 in Kyiv; Ukraine said it struck a solid rocket fuel plant in Russia's Rostov region (X story).

Diplomacy. ISW assesses the Kremlin is refusing to budge from its 2022 maximalist demands while ignoring Zelensky's offers of large-scale frontline ceasefires (ISW, 8:16 PM Sat). Lavrov said Zelensky is welcome in Moscow with security guarantees; Kyiv remains skeptical, and US-involved talks may resume in Abu Dhabi (X story). ISW called the humanitarian evacuation from occupied Oleshky, Kherson Oblast, "remarkably successful" (ISW, 6:12 PM Sat).

Other Conflicts

  • Gaza: Israeli strikes killed at least eight Palestinians Sunday, including a child, per local emergency services, with five killed near Al-Mawasi west of Khan Younis (X story, 6:53 AM). Toll per local sources; not independently confirmed.
  • Sudan: Sudan filed a UN Security Council complaint accusing Ethiopia of allowing drones to launch from its territory; a Darfur market strike killed at least 102 people, and the UN Secretary-General condemned the attacks (X story).
  • India: Five killed in fresh ethnic violence in Manipur state (Reuters, 4:45 AM).

Top World News

  • Germany: Masked rioters looted shops in Frankfurt Saturday as large demonstrations turned violent; police used water cannon against youths throwing Molotov cocktails (AFP, 5:04 AM). Interior Minister Dobrindt called for tough penalties (Reuters, 6:40 AM).
  • Binance: The exchange resumed bitcoin and ether withdrawals after covering most of the roughly $6.3 billion stolen in a hack the FBI attributed to North Korea (AFP, 4:02 AM; Reuters, 6:10 AM).
  • US midterms are Tuesday, Nov 3, with millions already voted (AFP, 5:38 AM).
  • Bolivia declared a state of emergency over El Niño impacts (AFP, 4:30 AM).
  • Malaysia Airlines says customers may be affected by the Amadeus data breach (Reuters, 3:00 AM).
