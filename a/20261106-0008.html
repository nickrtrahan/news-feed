Russia–Ukraine

  • Kyiv power and water. Kyiv Independent and X news summaries say water disruptions returned and metro service was briefly suspended amid power fluctuations after recent Russian strikes on the energy grid; some areas have faced long daily blackouts (Kyiv Independent, 2:48 PM ET; X news summary). Zelenskyy, speaking at the Ibero-American Summit in Madrid, highlighted strikes on grid equipment vital to nuclear plants and pressed partners for transformers and generators (Kyiv Independent, 11:39 AM ET).
  • Komyshuvakha toll at 43. The death toll from the Nov 2 Russian strike on a school used as an aid point in Zaporizhzhia region rose to 43 as the search continued (Kyiv Independent, 10:08 AM ET; X news summary).
  • Oleshky and Melania Trump. First lady Melania Trump hosted Ukrainian and Russian officials at the White House to mark a localized ceasefire and evacuation in Russian-occupied Oleshky, Kherson Oblast; Ukrainian officials say hundreds of civilians were evacuated and Ombudsman Dmytro Lubinets presented her Ukraine's Order of Princess Olga. ISW says the pause showed a local halt is possible but that Russian intransigence remains the barrier to broader talks (Kyiv Independent, 3:23 PM ET; ISW, 2:00 PM ET).
  • Aid and diplomacy. Zelenskyy said Spain will provide about $490 million in military aid focused on air defenses (Kyiv Independent, 5:22 PM ET). Jared Kushner pointed to Brussels talks on security guarantees and to Nov 10–11 U.S.–Ukraine–Russia working-level sessions in Abu Dhabi (X news summary). In a ZDF interview, Zelenskyy again rejected territorial concessions that would hand Russia land it could not take by force (X news summary). He also warned that some abducted Ukrainian children who have aged into adulthood are being sent into Russia's army—an allegation that needs more independent confirmation (X news summary).
  • Domestic Ukraine. Anti-corruption agencies charged senior law-enforcement figures in a sprawling scam-call-center case involving alleged bribery and violence (Kyiv Independent, 12:52 PM ET). A Ukrainian woman, Oleksandra Kazarovytska, died in illegal Russian detention in occupied Crimea after returning to help her parents, her school said (Kyiv Independent, 1:01 PM ET).
