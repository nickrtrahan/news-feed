World News Brief: Nov 18, AM

Top line

The US–Iran confrontation in the Gulf widened overnight. AFP reports that the US struck Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, and Kuwait and Bahrain both reported Iranian drone attacks. A gas tanker was set ablaze in the strait and its crew abandoned ship. In Ukraine, Russia fired 141 long-range drones overnight and hit central Odesa, while Kyiv released images of its strike that severed the Crimean Bridge. Trump has arrived in China ahead of a three-way summit with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin. All times are ET. Coverage runs from about 6:30 PM Tuesday, Nov 17, to 6:40 AM Wednesday, Nov 18.

Iran and Israel–Iran

  • US strike on Qeshm, attacks on the Gulf states. AFP reports that Gulf nations are on alert after the US struck Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz. Kuwait says it is countering "hostile drone attacks," and Bahrain says its air defenses intercepted attacks from Iran (AFP, 11:33 PM ET). Earlier, Bahrain's military accused Iran of ongoing attacks on the country (Al Jazeera, 8:38 PM ET), and Bahrain later issued an all-clear (Reuters, 8:50 PM ET). Al Jazeera describes the US target on Qeshm as an Iranian missile site and calls the exchange a "widening confrontation in the Gulf" (Al Jazeera, 4:00 AM ET). Unconfirmed: an X News summary says US Central Command reported all the drones aimed at US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain were intercepted with no damage, while Iran claims it hit Patriot systems. I didn't find a direct wire report of the CENTCOM statement.
  • Tanker hit in Hormuz. The crew of a liquefied petroleum gas tanker abandoned ship after a projectile struck it in the strait, and footage shows huge flames (Reuters, 12:45 AM ET; Reuters, 5:20 AM ET). ADNOC says one of its ships was attacked while crossing the strait Tuesday, with 11 crew injured and one missing (Al Jazeera, 2:26 AM ET). Al Jazeera says six oil tankers have reportedly been damaged by sea mines in the strait (Al Jazeera, 4:00 AM ET). An Atlantic Council analyst told Al Jazeera that US Navy escorts can't protect every ship passing through (Al Jazeera, 3:30 AM ET). Unconfirmed: an X News summary says the IRGC claimed the tanker strikes. I haven't seen that confirmed by a wire service.
  • Markets. Most Gulf stock markets fell as US–Iran hostilities escalated (Reuters, 4:20 AM ET).
  • "Dirty bomb" report. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said he doesn't think there is any imminent threat to Washington, responding to CNN's report on US intelligence about a possible Iranian dirty-bomb plot (Reuters, 12:15 AM ET; Times of Israel, 2:08 AM ET). He also said there's no evidence that recent tech outages were caused by anything "nefarious" (Al Jazeera, 10:20 PM ET).
  • Washington. In Manila, Trump said the US is "doing very well in Iran, to put it mildly" (Al Jazeera, 1:30 AM ET). At a Senate hearing, the Navy's leadership was criticized over the war's toll on sailors (Reuters, 1:20 AM ET). Navy secretary nominee Hung Cao said expired munitions were recoded and used against Iran during Operation Epic Fury (Al Jazeera, 7:10 PM ET).
  • Tehran. Iran's judiciary chief, Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Eje'i, said Iran is well prepared to face the US and that its army will not retreat (Al Jazeera, 3:50 AM ET).
  • Inside Iran. Unconfirmed: X News summaries report more anti-government protests in Abdanan, Ilam province, after a bus crash killed 13 oil workers, with chants supporting Reza Pahlavi. Israeli President Isaac Herzog and Pahlavi held a webinar on future ties with a democratic Iran. I found no wire report on the protests in this window.
  • Israel. Netanyahu met Naftali Bennett for a security briefing in the Knesset. Reports say Gadi Eisenkot was not informed (Times of Israel, 6:16 AM ET).

Russia–Ukraine

  • Overnight attacks. Russian attacks killed 5 people and injured 15 across Ukraine over the past day. Ukraine's Air Force says Russia launched 141 long-range attack drones overnight, 73 of them jet-powered, as it keeps up its assault on the energy grid (Kyiv Independent, 4:15 AM ET). A Russian strike on central Odesa killed 3 and injured 7 (Kyiv Independent, 5:38 AM ET).
  • Kyiv bridges. Traffic is at a standstill and commuters are walking across Kyiv after Russian strikes hit two key bridges over the Dnipro (AFP, 5:00 AM ET).
  • Crimean Bridge. Ukraine released images of the strike that severed the bridge to Crimea. The SBU says the joint operation destroyed one of the bridge's arches and a road span (AFP, 2:02 AM ET).
  • Allies split on deep strikes. A senior Pentagon official questioned how effective Ukraine's long-range strikes inside Russia are and warned that they could harden Russian resolve. Germany and Britain pushed back (Kyiv Independent, 10:57 PM ET).
  • Money and arms. According to a draft seen by the Kyiv Independent, the European Commission is set to propose an extra €45 billion in loans to Ukraine for 2027 (Kyiv Independent, 9:00 PM ET). EU leaders were holding a press conference on the Ukraine loan this morning (Reuters, 5:04 AM ET). An X News summary says the UK announced a £240 million package with more than 1,000 short-range air defense missiles, with deliveries starting in mid-December.
  • Other. A senior US soldier assigned to NATO, arrested in Britain last week on suspicion of spying for Russia, has been moved to US military custody (AFP, 2:59 AM ET). Ukraine protested the IAEA chief's plan to appoint a sanctioned Russian official as his deputy (Kyiv Independent, 7:20 PM ET). Reuters has an in-depth piece on how Ukraine is pairing ground robots, drones, and elite troops in "Operation Vivaldi" (Reuters, 2:40 AM ET).

Trump, Xi and Putin in Shenzhen

  • Trump arrived in China for the APEC summit, and Xi greeted him in person at the airport, a day before a three-way meeting with Putin (AFP, 4:27 AM ET; Reuters, 3:46 AM ET).
  • Xi and Putin held talks first. According to state media, Xi urged closer political and military ties with Russia (AFP, 3:41 AM ET). Both leaders criticized sanctions, and Xi pledged to stand up to trade protectionism (Reuters, 4:55 AM ET).
  • US limits on advanced AI chips for Chinese firms set the stage for the talks. Beijing is warning of retaliation, and few expect a breakthrough (Reuters, 5:35 AM ET). Russia staged what may be its largest military aircraft deployment across East Asia in more than a decade ahead of the meeting (Reuters, 4:30 AM ET). Taiwan says it is in "close contact" with the US (Reuters, 2:30 AM ET).

Other conflicts

  • Gaza. The US-backed Board of Peace warned the UN Security Council that the year-old Gaza ceasefire could collapse without immediate steps to carry out its terms (Al Jazeera, 9:20 PM ET). Hospital officials say at least two Palestinians were killed in separate Israeli drone strikes (Al Jazeera, 2:54 AM ET).
  • Yemen. The Houthis are threatening Aden after capturing territory along Yemen's Red Sea coast, which raises concerns for the Saudi-backed government and for shipping through the Bab el-Mandeb (Reuters, 12:35 AM ET). Unconfirmed: an X News summary says the Saudi-led coalition shot down a Houthi cruise missile aimed at Riyadh and struck 60 Houthi targets.
  • Sudan. The UN says drone strikes caused more than 80% of at least 1,390 civilian deaths it documented between January and June (Reuters, 1:15 AM ET).
  • DR Congo. The Ebola outbreak is spreading and killing at a record pace (Reuters, 5:30 AM ET), and the UN says insecurity is complicating efforts to contain it (Reuters, 1:05 AM ET). Fighting continues in the east despite rival peace efforts in Washington and Doha (Al Jazeera, 11:55 PM ET).

Other world news

  • Thousands rallied across Slovakia against Prime Minister Robert Fico's government, which is under fire for curbing rights and for its ties to Russia (AFP, 12:04 AM ET).
  • A magnitude 5.7 earthquake, Romania's strongest in years, was felt in Ukraine (Kyiv Independent, 4:58 AM ET).
  • Nvidia says it has stopped shipping its Hopper-series AI chips to China (AFP, 5:20 AM ET).
  • Reuters reports exclusively that the Trump administration is weighing whether to force US firms to disclose their use of Chinese AI (Reuters, 6:35 AM ET).
  • NASA's Voyager 1 has reached one light-day from Earth (AFP, 6:20 AM ET).
