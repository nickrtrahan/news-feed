World News Brief: Nov 21, AM

Top line

Saturday morning, Nov 21, covering roughly 6:40 PM Friday to 6:45 AM ET. More tankers were reported hit near the Strait of Hormuz, and CBS reported the US and Israel are weighing new strikes on Iran, while Tehran says it is readying tankers to test the US naval blockade. In Ukraine, a Russian double-tap strike on Kryvyi Rih killed at least two people, and Zelensky sharply rejected Trump calling Putin a "friend."

Iran and Israel–Iran

More tanker hits near Khasab. The UK Maritime Trade Operations agency reported two tanker attack incidents near Khasab, Oman, on Saturday (Reuters, 5:20 AM ET). One UKMTO warning said an outbound tanker was hit on the stern by an unknown projectile about 11 nautical miles north of Khasab at 0500 UTC (midnight ET), with no casualties (Straits Times, 3:02 AM ET). This follows Friday's projectile strike near Khasab that set a tanker on fire. Who fired the projectiles has not been confirmed, and it is unclear whether Saturday's two reports involve one ship or two.

New strikes under consideration. CBS News reported Friday that the United States and Israel are considering a new military assault on Iran in the near term, citing sources familiar with the matter; Israel is said to be concerned about Iran rebuilding missile sites and burying facilities underground (Iran International, citing CBS, 8:39 PM ET). No decision has been announced.

Diplomacy stalls. Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi said Iran wants diplomacy to end the war, while President Trump said Iran's terms are unacceptable (Reuters, 11:50 PM ET). A hardline lawmaker, Mojtaba Zolnouri, said Iran should wait for Washington to meet seven conditions before talks resume (Iran International, 1:23 AM ET).

Blockade showdown. Iranian lawmaker Vahid Ahmadi said Iran is preparing 14 oil tankers to challenge the US naval blockade and threatened retaliation against oil facilities across the region if any are attacked (Iran International, 3:31 AM ET). US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Friday that Iran has exhausted its seaborne oil supply and may never be paid for oil already sold (Iran International, 9:08 PM ET). A representative of the supreme leader at Kayhan newspaper urged Tehran to target undersea internet cables in the Persian Gulf to force an end to the blockade (Iran International, 1:35 AM ET).

Inside Iran. President Masoud Pezeshkian told investors Saturday that Iran is in a "silent war" on the economic front that is harder than the missile war (Iran International, 3:08 AM ET). Iran's Defense Ministry claimed it has expanded weapons production beyond pre-war levels, disputing US and Israeli assessments (Iran International, 1:49 AM ET). The judiciary warned Iranians abroad could be prosecuted and have assets frozen over online posts (Iran International, 2:12 AM ET). Videos sent to Iran International show pro-Pahlavi graffiti in Shiraz, Isfahan province and Tehran (Iran International, 5:49 AM ET). Those videos were not independently verified.

Region. CENTCOM commander Adm. Brad Cooper met Qatar's defense minister in Doha (Iran International, 6:16 AM ET). Saudi Arabia said it intercepted three Houthi missiles aimed at Riyadh (Reuters, 12:00 AM ET).

Russia–Ukraine

Kryvyi Rih double-tap strike. The number injured in Russia's double-tap strike on Kryvyi Rih rose to 47, including six children, two paramedics and two rescuers (Kyiv Independent, 10:58 PM ET). NEXTA later reported 58 injured and said a 7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old paramedic were killed (NEXTA, 1:48 AM ET). Overall, Russian attacks killed at least two and injured 83 across Ukraine; overnight Russia launched three ballistic missiles and 148 drones, Ukraine's Air Force said (Kyiv Independent, 4:09 AM ET).

Missiles over Poland. After Friday's massive Russian strikes, which killed at least seven in Kyiv, Poland accused Moscow of "provocation" after two Russian missiles likely landed on its territory (AFP, 7:00 PM ET). NEXTA reported that a Swedish Gripen was cleared by NATO to shoot down a Kh-101 cruise missile over Poland but never fired (NEXTA, 2:16 AM ET). That account is unconfirmed.

Zelensky vs. Trump on Putin. Zelensky called Putin "a killer" after Trump called him a friend (BBC, 11:35 PM ET). In an NBC interview he said Putin must understand the Crimean Bridge "will not stay" and warned Russia is increasing double-tap attacks (Kyiv Independent, 8:05 PM ET). Trump cited good talks with Putin on Ukraine but held back on security guarantees (Reuters, 8:10 PM ET).

Money and support. An EU budget proposal keeps Ukraine funding flat even as the war's cost rises (Kyiv Independent, 6:32 AM ET). Rep. Jim Himes, the incoming House Intelligence chairman, warned lawmakers could withhold SpaceX funding over Starshield restrictions in Ukraine, CNN reported (Kyiv Independent, 7:00 PM ET).

Other conflicts

Yemen. In Aden, divided government forces are preparing for a Houthi assault (Reuters, 7:50 PM ET).

Gaza. Unconfirmed by a major outlet in this window: local reports compiled on X say Israeli strikes killed at least three people across Gaza on Saturday despite the ceasefire, as winter flooding worsens shelter shortages.

World news

  • A magnitude 6.8 earthquake struck Peru's Andean region, sending Lima residents onto the streets (Reuters, 4:35 AM ET).
  • A fire at a Basra, Iraq, power station killed at least four and injured 30 (Reuters, 4:20 AM ET).
  • The FBI's criminal information database was reportedly hacked, CNN reports (Reuters, 5:40 AM ET).
  • Trump ordered federal contractors to notify the White House before giving information to Congress (Reuters, 5:45 AM ET).
  • Bitcoin fell to its lowest level in two years (Reuters, 6:00 AM ET).
