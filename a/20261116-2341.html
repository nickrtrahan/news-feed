World News Brief: Nov 16, PM

Top line

Iran says four oil tankers have hit mines or caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz in about 24 hours, and a British maritime agency reported another tanker attacked off Oman. Iran's foreign minister is in Oman for talks after Tehran proposed a two-day ceasefire. In Ukraine, Russia launched a large ballistic missile attack this evening, and Kyiv's mayor says missiles hit the city's North and South bridges. That came after Ukraine's record drone attack on Russia overnight and a reported strike on the Crimean Bridge. All times are ET. Coverage runs from about 6:45 AM to 6:40 PM Monday, Nov 16.

Iran and Israel–Iran

  • Tankers hit in Hormuz. Iran's navy says a fourth oil tanker caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after hitting a naval mine. That makes four tankers hit in 24 hours (Al Jazeera, 3:47 PM ET). Earlier, Iran's military said a tanker hit a mine and caught fire east of the strait (Al Jazeera, 7:45 AM ET). The IRGC says these ships were using a route Tehran calls "unauthorised" (Al Jazeera, 10:45 AM ET). These counts come from Iran and I haven't seen them independently confirmed.
  • Tanker attacked off Oman. The UK Maritime Trade Operations agency (UKMTO) says a tanker going through the strait was attacked 7 nautical miles off Khasab, Oman (Al Jazeera, 4:53 PM ET). It's not yet clear who did it.
  • Diplomacy. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi arrived in Muscat for talks with Oman on the region and shipping in Hormuz. Al Jazeera says the trip follows regional support for Tehran's proposed two-day ceasefire (Al Jazeera, 5:38 PM ET). Earlier, Araghchi said Iran trusts Pakistan's mediation with the US but admitted a deal will be hard (Al Jazeera, 7:03 AM ET). He also spoke by phone with Russia's Sergey Lavrov (Al Jazeera, 9:39 AM ET).
  • Air defense alerts. A security official in Isfahan says no hostile aircraft were detected in the city's airspace (Reuters, 5:10 PM ET). This followed posts showing flashes and air-defense fire over Tehran and Isfahan. Some locals blamed fireworks, according to an X News summary. Unconfirmed: what caused the flashes.
  • Threats to US officials. As Trump got ready for his Asia trip, he joked with reporters about reports of Iranian plots against senior US officials. Per an X News summary, CNN cited seven officials who said the plans are options rather than an imminent threat. A White House spokesperson said there are no credible threats to the homeland, and Iran denies the reports. Unconfirmed beyond those reports.
  • Home front and region. An analyst told Al Jazeera that Iran is warning of severe gas shortages this winter, made worse by the US naval blockade (Al Jazeera, 6:30 PM ET). Iran held another all-woman military training camp for volunteers in case mediation fails and fighting starts again (AP, 1:11 PM ET). A Bahraini court gave life sentences to dozens of Shi'ite clerics accused of plotting with Iran (Reuters, 10:50 AM ET). Sirens went off at Iraq's Khor Mor gas field after a drone flew over, sources told Reuters (Reuters, 5:45 PM ET).
  • Europe and Washington. EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas says the EU is considering sanctions on Iran over suspected cyberattacks on France (Al Jazeera, 11:28 AM ET). House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries called for an end to the war and blamed it for higher living costs (Al Jazeera, 8:50 AM ET). Iran's chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf criticized what he called "Israel-first politics" in the US (Al Jazeera, 2:19 PM ET).

Russia–Ukraine

  • Kyiv bridges hit. Russia launched a large ballistic missile attack across Ukraine. Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said early reports showed hits on the capital's North and South bridges (Kyiv Independent, 5:28 PM ET). Earlier, Zelensky told people to heed air-raid alerts tonight and tomorrow, and called North Korea's missile development "extremely dangerous" (Kyiv Independent, 4:30 PM ET). Damage and casualties are still being reported.
  • Crimean Bridge and refinery. The Crimean Bridge was hit in a reported attack and traffic was restricted. Russia also reported a record number of Ukrainian drones, with Moscow Oblast among the main targets. Ukraine's General Staff says it hit a key oil refinery in southern Russia, damaging three processing units (Kyiv Independent, 3:00 PM ET).
  • Drone surge. Ukraine's prime minister says Russia could launch 6,500 jet-powered Shahed-type drones at Ukraine in November. These drones are faster and harder to shoot down than the older propeller models (Kyiv Independent, 12:28 PM ET).
  • Nuclear risk. Nuclear safety regulators from 38 countries warned that the risk of a nuclear accident in Ukraine is rising this winter because Russia keeps attacking the power grid (Kyiv Independent, 12:01 PM ET).
  • French personnel in Ukraine. A source told AFP that French soldiers have been in Ukraine for months training Ukrainian troops and maintaining equipment, with no combat role (AFP, 1:15 PM ET).
  • Europe. Estonia summoned a Russian diplomat over an alleged sabotage attempt on a drone maker (Kyiv Independent, 11:21 AM ET). In France, presidential front-runner Marine Le Pen says she would end financial aid to Ukraine if elected in April 2027 (Kyiv Independent, 1:56 PM ET).

Other world news

  • North Korea–Russia. Kim Jong Un toured a tourist zone while a senior North Korean official visited Moscow (Reuters, 5:50 PM ET).
  • China summit. The BBC looks at China hosting world leaders amid its AI push ahead of this week's summit (BBC, 6:08 PM ET).
  • Europe migration. Austria says five EU countries are starting talks with Rwanda about a deportation hub (Reuters, 5:35 PM ET).
  • US. United Airlines reported a widespread system outage, just days after a nationwide ground stop (AP, 5:20 PM ET). The US is set to lose its measles-eliminated status this week as an outbreak keeps spreading (AFP, 5:45 PM ET).
  • Afghanistan. Reports say Taliban enforcers raided clinics and told male doctors not to treat women unless a male guardian is present, per an X News summary. Unconfirmed by a major wire in my search.
